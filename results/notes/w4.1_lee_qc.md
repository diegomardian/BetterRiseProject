# W4.1 — Lee QC: the mitochondrial cap is a no-op, the depth filter was not

**Owner:** W4 · **Week:** 5 · **Branch:** `w4/decisions-9-10-and-lee-qc`

Answers [`docs/open_decisions.md`](../../docs/open_decisions.md) #12's ask of W4
("run the same per-compartment `pct_mito` breakdown on Lee and set the cap from
it"). Code: [`src/estimator/ingest.py`](../../src/estimator/ingest.py),
[`src/estimator/lee_io.py`](../../src/estimator/lee_io.py).

Measured on all 91,103 annotated cells of GSE132465 (SMC, 63,689) and
GSE144735 (KUL3, 27,414) — the full deposits, not a pilot.

---

## Headline

**The mitochondrial cap was never the problem on Lee. The depth filter was, and
it cut SMC's tumour epithelium 29.6 points harder than its normal arm — in the
direction of the prior hypothesis.**

Both findings come from the same run, and the second was not what #12 asked for.

---

## 1 · The cap is inherited, not chosen

Both deposits are already filtered at 20% mitochondrial upstream. Observed
maxima: **19.995** (SMC), **19.994** (KUL3). The cap fails **zero** cells at any
value ≥ 20, and GEO does not ship the droplets that would let W4 revisit the
authors' cut.

| compartment | SMC median `pct_mito` | KUL3 median |
|---|---|---|
| **Epithelial cells** | **8.6%** | **9.7%** |
| Stromal cells | 4.1% | 5.1% |
| Myeloids | 4.6% | 4.9% |
| Mast cells | 5.4% | 5.1% |
| T cells | 3.7% | 4.3% |
| B cells | 2.7% | 3.3% |

Epithelium runs highest, as on GSE178341, and normal epithelium runs higher than
tumour (13.3% vs 8.4%, SMC) — W1's direction reproduces. But every number here
is **conditioned on the authors' 20% cut**, so none of them is comparable to
GSE178341's unconditioned 29.8% epithelial median.

`DEFAULT_MAX_PCT_MITO` stays **20.0**, and the constant now says why: it is what
the data already had done to it. W1's 50.0 and W4's 20.0 are not a disagreement
between workstreams — the deposits differ. Same shape as #11.

## 2 · Running the mandated retention check found the real bias

#12 requires no systematic tumour/normal retention gap before any compositional
number is believed. W4 had one:

| SMC epithelium | MAD pooled per study | MAD per study × compartment |
|---|---|---|
| normal arm retained | 88.5% | **100%** |
| tumour arm retained | **62.0%** | **100%** |
| median per-patient gap | **−29.6 pts** | **0.0 pts** |
| patients with a >10 pt gap | **9 of 10** | **0** |
| whole-cohort retention | 87.3% | 99.8% |

KUL3, same sign, milder: **−6.4 pts** median, 3 of 6 patients flagged.

### Mechanism

A MAD outlier rule assumes one unimodal population. A cohort matrix is six.

| compartment | SMC median UMIs |
|---|---|
| **Epithelial cells** | **18,724** |
| Stromal cells | 7,132 |
| B cells | 6,338 |
| Myeloids | 4,861 |
| T cells | 2,598 |
| Mast cells | 2,013 |

Epithelium runs **3.9×** deeper than the median immune compartment, and immune
cells are the majority — so they set the pooled median and a tight pooled MAD,
and the upper bound fires on epithelial cells for being epithelial. Tumour
epithelium is deepest of all and is cut hardest.

### Why the direction matters

Mature colonocytes are the deepest epithelial cells there are. Cutting the
tumour arm's deepest cells understates the tumour mature fraction, which
**inflates the apparent compositional loss** — toward the prior hypothesis,
produced by a QC parameter rather than by biology. This is the failure W1 raised
for the mitochondrial cap on GSE178341, reaching Lee through a different filter,
and it would not have been found by looking at the cap.

### Fix

MAD bounds are computed within **(study, compartment)**. `compartment` is a
required column, not an optional one — defaulting it away restores the artifact
silently. Grouping is deliberately **not** by tissue: that would equalise
retention by construction and hide the artifact rather than remove it, which is
the per-sample-quantile trap of #13 one filter earlier.

`ingest.differential_retention()` is the check itself, run on every
`load_lee_cohort` call and logged per flagged patient against the same
10-point threshold W1 uses.

## 3 · What the cohorts look like after QC

Panel targets + `stem_pole` markers, both arms, labelling restricted to
epithelium:

| | SMC (GSE132465) | KUL3 (GSE144735) |
|---|---|---|
| cells retained | 39,094 | 27,208 |
| patients with **both** arms | **10** of 23 | **6** of 6 |
| epithelial cells | 7,080 | 6,168 |
| border cells (no Kitagawa arm) | 0 | 9,357 |
| `MUC2` present in the gene index | **no** | **no** |

Two things the gate memo should carry. **The compositional arm on SMC rests on
10 paired patients**, not 23 — 13 are tumour-only. And **MUC2 is absent from
both Lee gene indices**, so the `opposite_lineage` axis runs on 3 of its 4
markers on this cohort, independently of the MUC2/TFF3 target collision in #1.
