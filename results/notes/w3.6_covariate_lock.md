**Owner:** W3 · **Weeks:** 4–5 · **Branch:** `w3/covariate-lock` ·

Config: [`config/covariate_set.yaml`](../../config/covariate_set.yaml).
Enforcement: [`src/bulk/covariates.py`](../../src/bulk/covariates.py).

The brief says the set is written down *before any survival model is run*. So
the config was committed `proposed`, and **`require_locked()` refused to let
W3.7 fit anything against it** until the owner confirmed and it was flipped to
`locked` in its own commit. A pre-specification that does not stop you fitting
is a document, not a lock. This one stopped the fitting, then was lifted on
purpose and on the record.

## What was confirmed, and what is still the team's

The six choices in §2 were confirmed by the W3 owner on 2026-08-18 and the
config flipped in commit 53442b0. W3.7 ran after that, never before.

**Three of them remain formally the team's, not W3's** — open decisions #4
(pool or stratify), #14 (plate) and #16 (which endpoint leads). W3 proposed
answers rather than leaving them blank, because leaving them blank blocked W3.7
and because each has evidence behind it. They are *implemented* in this config
and *not yet ratified* by the group. Overruling any of them is a config change
with its own commit and a stated reason — the mechanism is unchanged, the
default just moved from "blocked" to "running".

## 1. The one number that drove everything

Your six covariates cost **ten degrees of freedom**, not six — stage and site
are four-level factors costing three each.

Against that, the event counts are tight:

| Endpoint | Usable | Events |
|---|---|---|
| PFI | 603 | 156 |
| OS | 605 | 124 |
| **DSS** | 583 | **75** |

The conventional floor is **10 events per degree of freedom**. Applying the
full six-covariate set to DSS at complete case gives **59 events over 10 df —
5.9**, which is not a model anyone should report. This is what shaped the
proposal.

## 2. The six proposals

### (a) Purity is excluded from the clinical baseline — the biggest one

Purity is a tumour-content measure. It confounds analyses whose **predictor is
expression**. W3.7's baseline contains no expression at all — the brief is
explicit: *"clinical covariates only, no project variables, no expression."*

So purity is not a confounder there, and requiring it costs **65 patients and 19
PFI events** for no inferential gain. Excluding it is what lets every endpoint
clear the floor:

| Context | Endpoint | n | Events | df | Events/df | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| clinical baseline | **PFI** | 548 | 142 | 9 | **15.8** | ✅ |
| clinical baseline | **DSS** | 532 | 67 | 6 | **11.2** | ✅ |
| clinical baseline | **OS** | 550 | 110 | 9 | **12.2** | ✅ |
| expression models | PFI | 489 | 124 | 10 | 12.4 | ✅ |
| expression models | DSS | 474 | 57 | 7 | 8.1 | ❌ |
| expression models | OS | 491 | 97 | 10 | 9.7 | ❌ |

Purity stays in the locked set — it enters the moment expression does. The
config encodes this as two **contexts** rather than two covariate sets, so there
is still one lock.

### (b) DSS drops site, pre-specified now

Site costs three degrees of freedom for the weakest prior claim. Stage is the
strongest prognostic term and the W3.7 sanity check; MSI is pre-registered; age
and sex cost one each. Dropping site takes DSS from **7.3 to 11.2** events/df.

The point of pre-specifying it *here* is that choosing it after seeing a fit
would be choosing it after seeing the answer.

### (c) PFI leads. Invariant 9 is implemented unchanged — open decision #16

Invariant 9 says DSS and PFI primary, OS secondary, and the config says exactly
that. But the CDR's own notes recommend PFI and call DSS *"approximated"* for
COAD/READ, and PFI has twice the events. So the config adds `lead: true` on PFI:
the endpoint a headline result is stated on.

This does not change invariant 9 — that needs a PR and two approvals. It records
which primary endpoint carries the weight.

### (d) COAD/READ enter as strata, not a covariate — open decision #4

Rectal cancer usually receives neoadjuvant chemoradiation, so assuming COAD and
READ share a baseline hazard with a proportional shift between them is not
credible. **Stratifying gives each its own baseline and costs zero degrees of
freedom** — which at these event counts is not a small consideration.

It also handles #15: tissue source site is confounded with project at
**V = 0.971**, so stratifying by project absorbs most of the institution effect
too. Adding both would be near-collinear.

### (e) Plate is out of the clinical baseline, required for expression models — open decision #14

W3.4 found plate associated with more expression variance than any biological
variable (13.2% above its permutation null, against 4.2% for MSI) and confounded
with MSI at V = 0.21. That sounds like it belongs in the set.

**It does not — here.** Plate affects expression *measurement*, and these models
contain no expression. It is not a confounder of a clinical-only survival model.
The config requires it as a stratum or random effect in `expression_models`,
where it genuinely is one.

If you disagree, the cost of including it is severe: 29 levels against ~550
patients would consume every degree of freedom the cohort has.

### (f) ABSOLUTE is the primary purity; conflicting MSI is missing, not a level

ABSOLUTE is called from copy number, so it is not circular with an expression
outcome; ESTIMATE is the sensitivity. Never coalesced — W3.3 showed they
correlate at only r = 0.57 with a systematic 0.13 offset.

The 16 patients whose WGS and WXS files give contradictory MSI calls are treated
as **missing**, not as a third level. A fabricated label in the project's single
pre-registered subgroup variable is worse than a smaller n.

## 3. Also excluded, deliberately

| Variable | Why not |
|---|---|
| tissue source site | V = 0.971 with project; stratification already absorbs it |
| treatment_outcome_first_course | 48% coverage, and it is a *response* measure, not a record of treatment given |
| race | Available but not pre-registered, and not part of the question |

## 4. What is honestly still weak

**DSS is underpowered in the expression context** at 8.1 events/df, *before* the
deconvolution fraction is even added. It should be reported as a consistency
check on PFI, not as an independent result. The config says so in prose.

**OS in the expression context is 9.7** — marginal. It is secondary anyway.

**The MSI subgroup is 76 patients.** Coverage is fine at 98.6%; group size is the
constraint. It stays an estimate with an interval, as planned, never a test.

**Complete case, no imputation.** Missingness concentrates in stage and MSI, both
plausibly not missing at random, and imputing a pre-registered subgroup variable
is not something to do quietly. The cost is visible in the attrition table
`build_design` emits at every step.
