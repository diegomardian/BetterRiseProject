## 1. The CDR disagrees with invariant 9, and someone should know

The brief says to honour the CDR's own recommended-use flags. Its notes sheet
says, verbatim:

> "For clinical outcome endpoints, we recommend the use of **PFI** for
> progression-free interval, and **OS** for overall survival. [...] Given the
> relatively short follow-up time, PFI is preferred over OS."
>
> "**DSS is relatively accurate for CESC, PAAD, and UVM, and is approximated for
> other tumor types.**"

COAD and READ are "other tumor types". So the CDR recommends PFI and OS, and
calls DSS approximated for exactly our two diseases — while
[CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) invariant 9 makes DSS primary and OS secondary.

**These reconcile, and the reconciliation is the useful part.** The CDR derives
DSS as *dead **and** with tumour*, and says of it:

> "This is not a 100% accurate definition but is the best we could do with this
> dataset. Technically a patient could be with tumor but died of a car accident
> and therefore incorrectly considered as an event."

That is the same failure mode invariant 9 objects to in OS — non-cancer death
counted as an event — **reduced but not removed**. Invariant 9's reasoning holds:
DSS is better than OS here. It is just not clean, and the CDR authors say so.

**PFI is the endpoint the project and the CDR agree on.** It also has twice the
events. Lead with PFI, report DSS alongside it, keep OS secondary.

Invariant 9 is implemented as written. Changing it needs a PR and two approvals,
so this note records the tension rather than resolving it.

## 2. Exclusions are per endpoint, and nothing is dropped globally

A patient with no `tumor_status` has no DSS but a perfectly good PFI. Dropping
them from the table would shrink every analysis to satisfy the strictest one. So
the table carries `usable_<endpoint>` and `exclusion_<endpoint>` for each
endpoint, and the only global exclusion is the single redacted case.

This is invariant 1's principle applied to clinical data: *not estimable here* is
a state to record, not a row to delete.

**The four rules, in the order applied:**

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Redaction` non-empty | TCGA withdrew the case. Excluded from everything. |
| 2 | Event indicator missing | For DSS this means tumour status was unknown or discrepant, so "died of disease" is **undefined, not zero**. |
| 3 | Follow-up time missing | No interval to model. |
| 4 | Follow-up time ≤ 0 | 22 patients have exactly 0 days. Most Cox implementations drop these silently; here the count is visible. |

Reasons are mutually exclusive and account for every patient — asserted in
`test_exclusion_reasons_are_mutually_exclusive_and_total`, so the reconciliation
below cannot double-count.

### Reconciliation, 629 CDR patients

| Endpoint | Role | Usable | Redacted | No event | No time | Time ≤ 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DSS** | primary | **583** | 1 | 22 | 1 | 22 |
| **PFI** | primary | **603** | 1 | 0 | 1 | 24 |
| OS | secondary | 605 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 22 |
| DFI | not used | 238 | 1 | 390 | 0 | 0 |

**DFI is not usable.** 390 of 629 patients have no DFI, and stage IV patients are
excluded from it by construction. It is carried in the table and marked
`not_used` rather than being quietly omitted.

## 3. Events — and the number that constrains W3.7

On usable patients only:

| Endpoint | Usable | Events | Censored | Event rate | Median follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DSS** | 583 | **75** | 508 | 12.9% | 670 d |
| **PFI** | 603 | **156** | 447 | 25.9% | 607 d |
| OS | 605 | 124 | 481 | 20.5% | 672 d |
| DFI | 238 | 31 | 207 | 13.0% | 821 d |

> **DSS has 75 events. That is the binding constraint on the whole survival arm.**
>
> The locked covariate set (stage, age, sex, MMR/MSI, purity, site) is six
> variables but roughly **eleven degrees of freedom** once the categoricals are
> expanded — stage 3, site 3, MSI 2, plus age, sex and purity. At 75 events that
> is under 7 events per degree of freedom, below the conventional floor of 10.
>
> PFI's 156 events give ~14 per df and are comfortable. **This is a second,
> independent reason to lead with PFI**, and it is a fact W3.6 should have in
> hand before the covariate set is frozen.

## 5. Covariate completeness, for the 621 modellable patients

| Covariate | Annotated | Coverage | Levels |
|---|---|---|---|
| age | 621 | 100% | — |
| sex | 621 | 100% | 2 |
| site | 618 | 99.5% | 4 |
| MMR/MSI | 612 | 98.6% | 3 |
| stage | 601 | 96.8% | 4 |
| **treatment_outcome_first_course** | **298** | **48.0%** | 4 |

**Treatment is recorded for less than half the cohort.** The brief asked for
"treatment where recorded" — this is the answer. It is not usable as a model
covariate at 48% and should not be adjusted for; note also that
`treatment_outcome_first_course` is a *response* field, not a record of what
treatment was given, so it is a poor proxy for the neoadjuvant-chemoradiation
difference that motivates [decision #4](../../docs/open_decisions.md).

**Site and MMR/MSI come from `src/bulk/clinical.py`, the GDC pull built for
W3.4.** The CDR carries neither, and re-deriving them here would create a second
source of truth for variables that already have one.

**CDR and GDC agree on stage for every patient where both have a value — zero
disagreements.** That is a genuine cross-source check and it passed; the
comparison column is kept in the table so it stays checkable.

## 6. Method notes

TCGA writes missing data as bracketed sentinels — `[Not Available]`,
`[Discrepancy]`, `[Unknown]`, `[Not Applicable]`. Left in place they become
categorical levels and a Cox model will happily fit a coefficient for
"[Not Available]" stage. All are converted to NaN, with a parametrised test per
sentinel.

Stage is harmonised across AJCC editions by dropping sub-stage letters
(`Stage IIIB` → `III`), the same function W3.4 uses, with the raw value retained
as `stage_raw`.

Nothing here is a survival model. W3.7 runs those, after W3.6 locks the
covariate set.
