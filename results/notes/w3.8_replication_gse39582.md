# W3.8 — Independent replication in GSE39582

**Owner:** W3 · **Branch:** `w3/replication-gse39582` · **Scope:** beyond the
original brief, added because the W3.2 finding is about to redirect the project

Cohort: Marisa et al., French CIT consortium. **566 tumours + 19 normal mucosa**,
Affymetrix HG-U133 Plus 2.0. Code:
[`src/bulk/replication.py`](../../src/bulk/replication.py).

---

## Verdict: it replicates

**Bulk GUCA2A loss is continuous in an independent cohort measured on a
completely different platform.** Same for CDX2 and MS4A12. Not one of the 24
strata tested shows multimodality.

The strongest thing about this is what it rules out. GSE39582 is different
patients, a different country, a different collection era, and — critically —
**microarray rather than RNA-seq**, with entirely different noise behaviour,
dynamic range and detection floor. A result that survives that is not an artifact
of TCGA and not an artifact of sequencing.

| Gene | TCGA dip *p* | GSE39582 dip *p* | TCGA % of normal | GSE39582 % of normal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GUCA2A | 0.982 | **0.997** | 1.40% | **1.72%** |
| CDX2 | 0.969 | **0.987** | 94.7% | **84.8%** |
| MS4A12 | — | **0.992** | 0.82% | **0.35%** |

Dip test null is unimodality, so a large *p* means one group.

**The effect sizes agree to a degree I did not expect.** GUCA2A sits at 1.4% of
normal in TCGA and 1.7% here. CDX2 barely moves in both. MS4A12 is near-obliterated
in both. Two platforms that share no methodology arriving within a factor of two
on all three genes is a strong sign the underlying biology is being measured
rather than the instrument.

Percentages are computed from log2 differences of medians in both cohorts, which
is the only currency an array and an RNA-seq cohort share. TCGA figures use
log2-CPM; a TPM basis shifts CDX2 to ~100% and GUCA2A to ~1.25%, which is why
the basis is stated rather than assumed.

## The targeted test: CIMP+

This is what the cohort was worth the effort for. If a discrete off-state exists
anywhere, it should be in the **CpG island methylator phenotype** — promoter
hypermethylation is a switch, not a dial. TCGA's clinical data does not carry
CIMP status; this cohort does.

| Stratum | n | GUCA2A dip *p* | CDX2 dip *p* | MS4A12 dip *p* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CIMP+** | 91 | 0.804 | **0.515** | 0.785 |
| CIMP− | 405 | 0.999 | 0.979 | 0.992 |
| dMMR | 75 | 0.815 | 0.950 | 0.973 |
| pMMR | 444 | 0.995 | 0.966 | 0.972 |
| proximal | 224 | 0.959 | 0.983 | 0.992 |
| distal | 342 | 0.992 | 0.991 | 0.914 |

**No bimodality in the methylator phenotype either.** CDX2 in CIMP+ gives the
lowest *p* anywhere across both cohorts at 0.515 — and 0.515 is not a signal, it
is the middle of the null distribution.

This closes the most plausible remaining escape route for the two-group
hypothesis. It was worth checking precisely because a positive result there would
have been mechanistically coherent; a negative one is correspondingly
informative.

Note dMMR here is n=75 against TCGA's MSI n=76 — near-identical subgroup sizes,
so the two cohorts are underpowered in the same way and neither rescues the
other on that front.

## Two things this does *not* do

**It does not make the finding stronger than one method allows.** Both cohorts
were tested with the same dip test and the same mixture comparison, because
`run_replication` imports `premise.assess` rather than reimplementing it. That is
deliberate — a difference between cohorts cannot then come from a difference in
the analysis — but it also means a shared blind spot in the method would be
invisible in both.

**It does not make bulk able to answer the project's question.** Bulk still
cannot separate "the cells left" from "the cells went quiet". Replication makes
the premise-check finding trustworthy; it does not extend its reach.

## Invariant 4 was respected

The cohorts are **never pooled**. Each is estimated independently and reported
side by side in `replication_tcga_vs_gse39582.parquet`. The comparison table
reads TCGA's numbers from the committed W3.2 result rather than from anything
retyped here, so it cannot drift from the source.

## Method notes

54,675 probes → 42,986 with an unambiguous symbol → **21,655 unique genes**.
Probes tagged as multi-mapping (`PAX8 /// PAX8-AS1`) are dropped rather than
attributed to the first listed gene. Duplicate probes for one gene collapse to
the **highest-mean probe** — the same rule W3.3 uses for duplicate Ensembl IDs;
averaging would dilute a real signal with a dead probe.

The correctness risk in a GEO series matrix is positional: characteristics are
stored one line per field with values in sample column order and no per-sample
key, so a misalignment gives every patient someone else's MMR status without
anything raising. `test_characteristics_stay_attached_to_their_own_sample` is the
guard, and it is the test to look at first if this module is ever changed.

The 19 normal-mucosa samples fall below the n=20 gate and correctly report
`insufficient_n` rather than a number that would look like an answer — the same
guard that fired for TCGA's READ normals.

12 new tests, 552 in the full suite, `ruff` clean.

## A correction to the earlier notes

W3.2 and the plain-language report described GUCA2A as "roughly 64-fold" lower in
tumours. On a log2-CPM basis it is **1.40% of normal, i.e. ~71-fold**; on TPM
medians ~80-fold. The stable way to state it across cohorts and transforms is
**"under 2% of the level in healthy tissue"**, true in both TCGA (1.4%) and
GSE39582 (1.7%). The earlier figure was in the right region but the basis was
not stated, and it moves depending on the transform.
