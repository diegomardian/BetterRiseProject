These aggregates come from a separate source-substitution and interval study.
The diagnosis rates are null sensitivity experiments with B=2000 and 2000 studies
per row. Distribution substitutions and arm-size changes do not isolate causal
shares. At 800 cells the two designs coincide; Gaussian rates reuse standardized
random draws across cohort/pool conditions.

In the repair table, draw_reference_inclusion is NOT fixed-pool coverage.
source_verdict reproduces the original classification: its null threshold is
5% + 2*Monte Carlo SE; the classifier does not test alternative coverage.
It is not a certificate of nominal calibration. Rejection and detection rates
condition on returned intervals; abstention columns give the missing fraction.
Patient-t equally weights shared patients and changes the cell-weighted target.
The main paper's five-method fixed-pool comparison is a separate experiment.
