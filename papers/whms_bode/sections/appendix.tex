\paragraph{Precision follow-up and comparator.}\label{app:precision}
Five fresh seed streams (202609160--202609164), each with 1{,}000 studies
per setting, provide 5{,}000 draws at each of four counts and two shifts in
four cohort/pool combinations. Every draw uniformly chooses a two-patient
pair, samples 800 reference and $n_T$ tumor mature-cell counts independently
with replacement, and thins tumor counts binomially. This is the original
generator's marginal law for this contrast, not a replay of its random stream.
All 120 source-pair summaries match the earlier analysis. The vectorized
percentile routine agrees with the original implementation on 32 actual-pool
checks; the Welch implementation agrees with an independent library routine.

Writing $v_a=S_a^2/n_a$ for arm $a$, the Welch interval is
$\hat\theta\pm t_{0.975,\nu} f_N\sqrt{v_N+v_T}$, where
$\nu=(v_N+v_T)^2/[v_N^2/(n_N-1)+v_T^2/(n_T-1)]$.
Zero estimated total variance would give an unavailable comparator interval;
none occurred. For the sparsity diagnostic, sample-based standard error is
compared with $f_N\sqrt{\sigma_P^2/n_N+
(s^2\sigma_P^2+s(1-s)\mu_P)/n_T}$. Aggregate outputs retain signed
rejections, widths, seed-specific rates and paired differences. The settings
and single comparator were fixed before this follow-up was run; it remains an
exploratory study on previously examined source data. The earlier 200-draw
target diagnostic is retained in the supporting summaries.


\section{Calibration details and limits}
\label{app:gridcaveats}

\paragraph{Historical grids.}\label{app:historical}
Before the controlled run, SMC reference-only binned candidates were 100,
90 and 70 on the committed, filled and dense grids, with 500, 500 and 200
replicates per setting. These runs changed random streams and bin boundaries
as well as evaluated counts; Section~\ref{sec:calibration} separates those
choices. KUL3 reference-only candidates were 400 or 800, and no pooled-draw
candidate qualified. Its largest setting sampled 800 mature cells per arm
with replacement from 844 distinct source cells, not additional patients.

\paragraph{Recovery accounting and unavailable contrasts.}\label{app:generator}
The four diagnostic sweeps comprise 1{,}711{,}200 replicates: 1{,}283{,}400 with
nonzero requested intrinsic reference and 427{,}800 with zero requested
reference, for which ratios are undefined. The first 50-replicate sweep has
119{,}600 draws, partitioned into 89{,}700 and 29{,}900, respectively. The
maximum estimate--realized-reference residual is zero throughout. At a
zero-cell setting the raw ratio is $1/(1-s)$ when the denominator is nonzero.
These raw diagnostic values arise from the harness setting an empty-arm mean
to zero; they do not define an effect in absent cells. The reporting gate
abstains and the interval is undefined. Separately, undefined intervals were
previously counted as failures through comparisons with \texttt{NaN}; corrected
rates exclude them and report abstentions. An all-abstaining bin has no
coverage estimate. One historical seed gives $ok=wide=45$, showing that a
three-state specification need not yield three distinct fitted regions.

\paragraph{Biological scope and patient uncertainty.}\label{app:guards}
\label{app:modal}\label{app:limitations}
The finest annotation resolution yields abstention in 28/28 patients under one
population definition and 24/28 under the other; coarser definitions do not
resolve identifiability. The coarsest definition labels every epithelial cell
mature and forces the compositional term to zero. In a separate comparability
analysis over three cohorts and 42 patients, 83 of 84 housekeeping-detection
control rows pass a 0.10 tolerance despite expression changes of 1.19--1.64
fold. Patient-bootstrap log$_2$ expression intervals cross the accepted
$[-0.5,0.5]$ boundaries in every cohort, leaving the comparisons undecided.
Depth matching discards roughly three quarters of the cells and pushes about
half the remaining estimable rows below the count threshold. Within SMC, changing tissue eligibility changes the mature-cell pool from
662 to 3{,}824 cells. The across-cohort comparison in
Section~\ref{sec:cohortgap} instead holds tissue eligibility fixed.
These observations restrict biological interpretation. Repeated cell draws and
overlapping patient pairs do not supply independent patient replication.

\section{Synthetic external-control design}
\label{app:eca}

The fixed registry contains 50{,}000 simulated records with stratum shares
$(0.55,0.30,0.15)$, baseline outcomes $Y_0\mid g\sim
\mathcal N(\mu_g,3^2)$, $\mu=(12,8,4)$, and effects
$Y_1-Y_0=\delta_g r$, $\delta=(3,1.5,0.25)$,
$r\sim\mathrm{Gamma}(4,0.25)$ (shape, scale). Responsiveness is fixed per record
and hidden from estimators. Enrollment weights favor shares $(0.15,0.25,0.60)$;
both arms sample without replacement and are disjoint. The pooled target is a
mixture of enrolled and control records, not the registry population itself.
Normal outcomes can be negative: this is an abstract continuous-outcome
experiment, not a fitted progression-free-survival model.

There are 200 study draws at each of $n=50,100,200,400,800,1600$,
using one seed (20260915) and one fixed registry: 1{,}200 studies, eight
estimators and five references yield 48{,}000 comparisons: 47{,}969 finite
values and 31 exclusions from one study with an empty stratum arm at $n=50$.
The registry uses the same seed. The intended finite-cohort truth averages
both potential outcomes over enrolled records; it is available to the
simulator, not to estimators observing one outcome per record. Five case-mix
shifts and 50 balance draws at $n=1600$ provide controls. Threshold crossings
in the saved shift table compare medians, not replicate-level decisions.

The nine prespecified falsification checks did not fire. They verify the
constructed comparison, not clinical realism or the frequency of the
validation mistake.

\section{The retained-gene control}
\label{app:controls}
The control-panel audit compares saved unmatched and depth-matched single-cell
summaries with a separate bulk dataset. Where both mature fractions equal
one (all epithelium, without marker-based subdivision), MS4A12 still changes
by $-0.967$ to $-1.000$ across the tested cohort/matching strata; CDX2 on the
same cells changes by $-0.396$ to $-0.293$. Depth-matched MS4A12 changes are
$-0.971$ to $-0.960$. These observations weaken a uniform labeling or depth
artifact explanation; they do not eliminate every measurement artifact.
Across nondegenerate strata, the median baseline is 2.56 CP10K and tumor
expression 0.104 CP10K. Strata classified as degenerate have a median of one
relevant tumor cell and cannot establish a precise complete loss.

In GSE39582 (585 bulk samples), the saved MS4A12 log$_2$ fold change is
$-8.18$. This corroborates tissue-level depletion on a separate platform;
it does not identify a within-cell mechanism. Lower-abundance genes retain
more signal in the single-cell summaries, arguing against a simple universal
abundance-floor explanation, not proving absence of gene-specific dropout.
Repository chronology places panel freezing before dataset registration;
it cannot establish what evidence was known outside the repository. The
panel's prior expectation of retention therefore needs reassessment.

\section{External audit and research context}
\label{app:prevalence}

\paragraph{Search and classification.}
A pre-registered queue contained 41 entries; 19 were screened out and seven
classified within the 26 entries examined in order. An amendment before code
inspection relaxed unsuccessful queries; the classification rule was unchanged.
Generating-parameter references or quantities computed outside the estimator's
observed records give \textsc{no}. Same-record references require examination
of inputs, weights, conditioning, nuisance fitting and observation process;
\textsc{yes} requires affirmative evidence of functional identity. All seven
recorded verdicts are \textsc{no}: \texttt{causl}, target-trial-emulation,
\texttt{ecasim}, PlasmodeSimulation, StatsPAI, \texttt{diff-diff}, and
Auto-Empirical-Research-Skills. The first six use generating parameters or
outside-sample quantities; the last compares cross-fitted partialling-out with
full-sample OLS. Its $4.0\times10^{-14}$ residual on noiseless linear data
becomes 0.125 after adding outcome noise. The functions agree on the restricted
noiseless support but not on general noisy datasets; the \textsc{no} verdict
concerns shared functional form. Classification used one reader, only this last
repository was executed, and the sample is nonrandom. The virtual-control-arm
query located no examinable code. These limits preclude a prevalence estimate.

\paragraph{Related scope.}\label{app:relatedwork}
Cell-count filters are established in pseudobulk analysis \citep{crowell2020};
scCODA, Milo, propeller and DA-seq address composition
\citep{buettner2021,dann2022,phipson2022,zhao2021}, while cacoa separates
composition and expression changes \citep{petukhov2022}. Selective prediction
motivates evaluating both answers and abstentions
\citep{chow1970,elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. Conformal prediction coverage
\citep{vovk2005,angelopoulos2023} does not define a missing conditional
population or automatically provide intervals for this intrinsic parameter.

\paragraph{Documentary source of the case.}\label{app:provenance}
The anonymous review record includes the original validation statement
(Artifact A), interval criteria (Artifact B), and numerical context. Exact
source mapping and checksums remain outside the review bundle. This records
one internal validation error; the external search found no positive case.

\paragraph{Implementation and provenance.}\label{app:withdrawn}
Corrections separated collapsed population definitions, aligned labeling,
tracked producing scripts, and repaired undefined-interval denominators.
Saved tables retain commit hashes and seeds; these establish traceability,
not statistical validity.
