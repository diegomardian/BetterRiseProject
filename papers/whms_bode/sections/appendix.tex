\paragraph{Precision follow-up and comparator.}\label{app:precision}
Five fresh seed streams (202609160--202609164), each with 1{,}000 studies
per setting, provide 5{,}000 draws at each of four counts and two shifts in
four cohort/pool combinations. Every draw uniformly chooses a two-patient
pair, samples 800 reference and $n_T$ tumor mature-cell counts independently
with replacement, and thins tumor counts binomially. This reproduces the generator's marginal law;
all 120 source-pair summaries match. Percentile and Welch implementations
agree with independent checks.

Writing $v_a=S_a^2/n_a$ for arm $a$, the Welch interval is
$\hat\theta\pm t_{0.975,\nu} f_N\sqrt{v_N+v_T}$, where
$\nu=(v_N+v_T)^2/[v_N^2/(n_N-1)+v_T^2/(n_T-1)]$.
Zero estimated total variance would give an unavailable comparator interval;
none occurred. For the sparsity diagnostic, sample-based standard error is
compared with $f_N\sqrt{\sigma_P^2/n_N+
(s^2\sigma_P^2+s(1-s)\mu_P)/n_T}$. Settings were fixed before this exploratory follow-up; aggregate exports retain
widths, seed rates and paired differences.

\paragraph{Reporting-band and repair follow-up.}\label{app:repair}
Five fresh streams (202609170--202609174), 400 studies each, use the same
pool-conditional sampling law at 30 and 50 cells. For BCa, bias correction uses midpoint ties;
acceleration uses signed two-sample influence contributions
$-(Y_{Ni}-\bar Y_N)/n_N$ and $(Y_{Ti}-\bar Y_T)/n_T$.
Endpoints agree with SciPy's independent two-sample jackknife implementation
on continuous, discrete, sparse and zero-tumor samples. Bootstrap-$t$ uses
$t^*=(\hat\theta^*-\hat\theta)/\widehat{\mathrm{SE}}^*$ and reversed pivot
quantiles, with Welch standard errors recomputed in each resample. Invalid
BCa transforms or any undefined studentized pivot return an unavailable
interval; none occurred. All 160{,}000 intervals are finite. The study settings
were fixed before the run; this remains exploratory analysis on existing pools.

At least one adjusted BCa endpoint lies within the outermost ten bootstrap
order positions in 5.2--56.3\% of studies across settings. These finite-budget
results therefore do not characterize BCa with arbitrarily precise tail
quantiles. Seed rates, paired differences and numerical diagnostics accompany
the aggregate summaries.

\paragraph{Source substitutions and clustered alternatives.}\label{app:substitution}
The separately registered diagnosis crosses two counts (50, 800), four
cohort/pool conditions, four source families and two arm-size designs.
Each of its 64 rows uses five streams of 400 draws, 2{,}000 inner resamples
and the same two-patient pools; all intervals are defined. The families are
empirical counts, mean-rescaled positive counts, mean/skew-matched gamma
(with some skew clipping), and mean/variance-matched Gaussian. All rates
were reproduced from source data. Gaussian rows reuse standardized draws
across cohorts. Substitution contrasts are not additive causal shares or
lower bounds on a skew contribution. At 800 cells the designs coincide;
empirical null rejection is 4.55--6.40\%.

\paragraph{Patient-$t$ target check.}\label{app:patienttarget}
We replay the incoming patient-$t$ studies at 50 and 800 cells, two and five
held-out patients, both shifts and all four cohort/pool conditions: 64{,}000
studies (five streams of 400 per setting). Returned intervals, rejection,
abstention and sampled-reference inclusion counts match all 32 original
settings exactly. The two fixed targets use either the mean of all eligible
source cells or the equally weighted mean of source-patient means, computed
before drawing arms. Both condition on the selected pool. Patients lacking
mature source cells are outside the latter mean; incomplete patient pairs
can make the interval unavailable. At 50 cells and two held-out patients,
equal-patient coverage is 96.1--97.6\%. Exports include all counts, target-specific
coverage, Wilson intervals, availability and unconditional detection.

\section{Calibration details and limits}
\label{app:gridcaveats}

\paragraph{Historical grids.}\label{app:historical}
Earlier reference-only SMC cutoffs were 100, 90 and 70 under grids with
500, 500 and 200 replicates. They changed streams, bins and budgets together;
Section~\ref{sec:calibration} uses shared draws to separate grid effects.
KUL3's largest setting resampled 800 mature cells per arm from 844 distinct
source cells, not additional patients.

\paragraph{Recovery accounting and unavailable contrasts.}\label{app:generator}
Across 1{,}711{,}200 diagnostic replicates, 427{,}800 have zero requested
reference and undefined recovery ratios. Estimate--realized-reference
residuals are zero. At zero cells, raw diagnostic values arise from setting
an empty-arm mean to zero; they define no effect in absent cells. The
reporting gate abstains. Corrected interval rates exclude undefined outputs
and report availability; an all-abstaining bin has no coverage estimate.
The saved summaries retain the full sweep accounting.

\paragraph{Biological scope and patient uncertainty.}\label{app:guards}
\label{app:modal}\label{app:limitations}
Across three cohorts and 42 patients, patient-bootstrap expression intervals
cross the accepted log$_2$ boundaries $[-0.5,0.5]$, leaving comparability
undecided. Depth matching discards about three quarters of cells. Changing
SMC tissue eligibility changes the mature-cell pool from 662 to 3{,}824;
Section~\ref{sec:cohortgap} holds eligibility fixed.

\section{Synthetic external-control design}
\label{app:eca}

For enrolled records $E$ and controls $C$, the target is
$\tau_E=|E|^{-1}\sum_{i\in E}\{Y_i(1)-Y_i(0)\}$.
The estimator averages stratum contrasts using either enrolled shares
$n_{Eg}/|E|$ or pooled shares $(n_{Eg}+n_{Cg})/(|E|+|C|)$.
At zero case-mix shift, pooled-standardization bias against $\tau_E$ is
$-0.002$ units. Under the full shift, between-arm balance coexists with a
maximum standardized difference of 0.964 from the enrolled population.


The registry has 50{,}000 records with stratum shares $(0.55,0.30,0.15)$,
$Y_0\mid g\sim\mathcal N(\mu_g,3^2)$, $\mu=(12,8,4)$,
and $Y_1-Y_0=\delta_g r$, $\delta=(3,1.5,0.25)$,
$r\sim\mathrm{Gamma}(4,0.25)$ (shape, scale), hidden from estimators.
Enrollment favors $(0.15,0.25,0.60)$; disjoint arms sample without replacement.
These are abstract continuous outcomes. The pooled population combines
trial and control records.

One seed (20260915) generates the registry and 200 studies at each of
$n=50,100,200,400,800,1600$. Eight estimators and five references give
48{,}000 comparisons: 47{,}969 finite, with 31 exclusions from one empty-stratum
study at $n=50$. Five case-mix shifts and 50 balance draws at $n=1600$
provide controls. Saved threshold crossings compare medians, not individual
decisions. All nine prespecified falsification checks pass.

\section{The retained-gene control}
\label{app:controls}
The control-panel audit compares saved unmatched and depth-matched single-cell
summaries with a separate bulk dataset. Where both mature fractions equal
one (all epithelium, without marker-based subdivision), MS4A12 still changes
by $-0.967$ to $-1.000$ across the tested cohort/matching strata; CDX2 on the
same cells changes by $-0.396$ to $-0.293$. Depth-matched MS4A12 changes are
$-0.971$ to $-0.960$. These observations weaken a uniform labeling or depth
artifact explanation; they do not eliminate every measurement artifact.
Across nondegenerate strata, the median baseline is 2.56 CP10K and tumor
expression 0.104 CP10K. Strata classified as degenerate have a median of one
relevant tumor cell and cannot establish a precise complete loss.

In GSE39582 (585 bulk samples), the saved MS4A12 log$_2$ fold change is
$-8.18$. This corroborates tissue-level depletion on a separate platform;
it does not identify a within-cell mechanism. Lower-abundance genes retain
more signal in the single-cell summaries, arguing against a simple universal
abundance-floor explanation, not proving absence of gene-specific dropout.
Repository chronology places panel freezing before dataset registration;
it cannot establish what evidence was known outside the repository. The
panel's prior expectation of retention therefore needs reassessment.

\section{External audit and research context}
\label{app:prevalence}

\paragraph{Search and classification.}
Of 41 queued entries, 26 were examined: 19 screened out and seven classified.
A pre-inspection amendment relaxed unsuccessful queries without changing the
rule. All verdicts were \textsc{no}: six use generating/outside-sample references;
the seventh's near-zero residual on noiseless data becomes 0.125 with noise.
One reader classified this nonrandom sample and executed only the seventh
repository. No examinable virtual-control-arm code was found. The search
supports no prevalence estimate.

\paragraph{Related scope.}\label{app:relatedwork}
Cell-count filters \citep{crowell2020}, composition analysis (scCODA, Milo, propeller and cacoa)
\citep{buettner2021,dann2022,phipson2022,petukhov2022}, selective prediction \citep{chow1970,elyaniv2010,geifman2017}
and conformal coverage \citep{vovk2005,angelopoulos2023} address related tasks;
none automatically validates this intrinsic-effect interval or its reporting rule.
