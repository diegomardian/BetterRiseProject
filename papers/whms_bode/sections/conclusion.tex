The audit separates three claims that had been conflated: recovering a
reference value, covering the intended parameter, and meeting the requirements
of a reporting rule. Sharing the sampled reference mean changes the error
variance being assessed. Direct evaluation of the fixed-pool target finds
undercoverage at the halving and still worse coverage at the null.
Increasing the bootstrap budget and using skew-aware intervals improve some
conditions but do not qualify either tested reporting count.

For this artifact, the practical action is to withdraw the claim that 50 cells
is a validated reporting boundary. Outputs can be retained as exploratory
estimates with unvalidated uncertainty; a warning about width is not a
calibration guarantee. A replacement needs an explicit acceptable error rate,
adequate detection and independent assessment across its intended reporting
range. New patient-level evidence is needed before extending these conditional
results to clinical decisions.
