Direct evaluation does not support the original cell-count rule's recorded
justification. The pooled simulation yields no qualifying candidate; reference-
only candidates depend on cohort and aggregation, with additional sensitivity
to patient omission and bootstrap budget. The evaluation uses a draw-dependent
reference and does not certify fixed-population coverage or a replacement
clinical threshold.

The equality audit answers a separate question: whether the estimator reproduces
a specified empirical functional. Our simulations show that this can hold for
valid estimators and depends on the reference, trial design and observation
process. It neither causes the calibration failure nor establishes a flaw in
an estimator. Report target error, interval performance and abstention directly;
use functional-equivalence checks to make the scope of recovery evidence
explicit. No new patient outcome model or biological mechanism is validated
by these results.
