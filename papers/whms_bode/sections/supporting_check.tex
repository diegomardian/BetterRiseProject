\section{Supporting estimator and trial checks}
\label{app:matrix}\label{sec:matrix}

\paragraph{Reference dependence.}
With nonempty stratum arms, saturated G-computation \citep{robins1986}, inverse-probability weighting
(IPW) using empirical stratum propensities, non-cross-fitted saturated
augmented IPW (AIPW) and
matching using every control all reproduce
$T(D)=\sum_g(n_g/n)(\bar Y_{1g}-\bar Y_{0g})$. The IPW weights recover each
stratum mean; the AIPW augmentation sums to zero within stratum. Ordinary least squares (OLS) with
additive stratum indicators instead weights contrasts by normalized
$n_g\hat p_g(1-\hat p_g)$ \citep{angrist1998,frisch1933}. These known identities
show why the reference determines what an exact recovery check establishes.

\paragraph{Learned-generator check.}\label{app:learned}
Seven generators comprise four Gaussian mixtures, a multilayer-perceptron
(MLP) conditional Gaussian and two parametric controls. Training cohorts
are simulated from a three-stratum model with confounded treatment assignment
and a homogeneous additive effect of 3; learned generators are fitted to
these cohorts, not to clinical data. Ten estimators are then applied to fresh
synthetic cohorts from each fitted generator. Six sizes ($100,200,500,1000,2000,5000$), six seed streams
and 30 replicates schedule 75{,}600 evaluations on 7{,}560 cohorts;
generator failures leave 75{,}580 evaluations on 7{,}558 cohorts. Of these,
74{,}726 estimate--drawn-reference pairs are finite. The 854 exclusions
include 410 nonfinite references and 731 nonfinite estimates, overlapping
in 287 evaluations.

Saturated G-computation has zero residual against $T(D)$ in all 42
generator--size settings because the equality holds on every evaluable
synthetic dataset. It does not depend on learning the generator correctly.
Nonsaturated ridge and MLP outcome fits can depart; the largest MLP residual
under mixture generators is 3.3 outcome units. These checks concern the
estimator--reference pair, not realism of learned patient trajectories.
