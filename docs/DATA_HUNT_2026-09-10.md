# External data hunt, 2026-09-10 — what exists outside this repository

**Scope.** A literature and repository search for (a) a replication substrate for
avenue A, (b) a non-transcript identity or silencing label, (c) an instrument
positive control to replace the one §6g could not run, and (d) anything that
speaks to the project's premise from outside single-cell transcription.

**Status of each claim below is marked.** `VERIFIED HERE` means it was computed
in this session against a committed table or a live public API and the command is
given. `REPORTED` means it comes from a paper or a portal description and has not
been checked against the data. Nothing here is an analysis result and nothing
here licenses one; every item that could become an analysis names its gate.

1. **`Not Stated` and `Not stated` are two strings for one category.** A naive

2. **Uninformative is 12 of 35 for `ADVANCED` (34%), not 9.** The two spellings

3. **The missingness is not random, and it tracks the label it would stratify.**
   `ADVANCED` is uninformative for **5 of 11 `SER`** lesions against **1 of 16
   `AD`**, and for all three lesions whose attribute is absent
   (`POLYP_TYPE = Unknown`). Conditioning on advanced status drops serrated
   lesions preferentially — the same shape as D2 §6a, where ABSOLUTE dropped 107
   participants whose GUCA2A was lower than those it kept. Any use of this field
   declares that and reports complete-case attrition by arm.

**Three consequences.**

1. **`NEXT_AVENUES.md` item 3 (AD versus SSL) no longer needs Synapse to be
   sized, and for the intrinsic term it does not need Synapse at all.** The
   histopathology label is a different assay in a different modality from the
   transcripts, so it passes invariant 11, and it covers **13 versus 9 patients**
   after the conflicted-patient rule, rather than 5 versus 4. **It does not have equal standing to the WES label on

2. **Certification is still worth chasing for the compositional term, and not

   buys a *cleaner descriptive contrast*, not a test. It may recover more than
   the 13 of 35 cBioPortal covers, since cBioPortal's 30 sequenced samples are
   not the same set as the 35 exact VCF matches, but the ceiling is 35 lesions
   across 29 patients.
3. **The two labels cross-check each other outcome-blind.** Over all 30
   sequenced samples: APC-truncating is 12/15 AD and 1/19 SER; BRAF V600E is
   10/19 SER and 0/15 AD. Agreement between an independent morphological call
   and an independent DNA call, computable before any transcript is read.

### 1a. Invariant 11 passes, and passing is not sufficient

**This qualifies consequence 1 and was raised in review of this document's first
draft.** Invariant 11 tests **provenance**: it refuses a label derived from the
transcripts whose programme is then claimed. The pathology label is not so
derived, so it passes cleanly. What invariant 11 cannot test is whether two
modalities are **measuring the same underlying tissue property**.

A pathologist's `AD` versus `SER` call reads crypt architecture and the
maturation of epithelium toward the luminal surface. The **compositional** term
of the decomposition is the change in mature-cell fraction. Those are largely
the same tissue property in two modalities, so an arm difference in the
compositional term risks being close to definitional — lesions selected on
architecture, then measured for architecture.

**The confound is graded, and the ranking is:**

| label | compositional term | intrinsic term |
|---|---|---|
| `ATYPIA` / `ADVANCED` | **worst** — dysplasia grading scores nuclear features and *failure of surface maturation* directly | acceptable |
| `POLYP_TYPE` (AD/SER) | **confounded** — architecture in, architecture out | acceptable |
| truncating `APC` / `BRAF` V600E | **clean** — a genotype is upstream of morphology and does not mechanically fix a mature-cell fraction | acceptable |

The intrinsic term is per-cell output *within* mature cells, which is not what a
pathologist grades, so no pathology label is circular against it.

**What this changes.** Under pathology labels the intrinsic contrast is a test
and the compositional contrast is **descriptive**. Under genotype labels the
compositional contrast is clean but sits at 5 versus 4 patients, which
`docs/HANDOFF.md` §3a says cannot carry an interval this project would report —
so it is descriptive too, for an unrelated reason. **Neither route yields a
compositional test.** Recording that before the run is the point.

**Gates before any of this is an analysis.** (i) HTAN/cBioPortal data-use terms
reviewed and recorded the way `docs/` records the UniToPatho CC-BY review;
(ii) `POLYP_TYPE`/`POLYP_SUBTYPE` provenance confirmed as pathologist diagnosis
rather than anything derived from the deposited transcripts — the attribute name
is not proof; (iii) a patient-level rule fixed in advance for the patients
carrying two lesions of different type; (iv) an arm-size and precision rule
pre-committed before any subgroup estimate, given `docs/HANDOFF.md` §3a on
intervals at n≈10; (v) invariant 4 — this is a within-study subgroup contrast and
is not pooled with anything.

## 2. The published mechanism literature contradicts the reason B1 was closed

`REPORTED`, and it is a direct disagreement with a committed conclusion, so it is
first among the literature items.

`docs/NEXT_AVENUES.md` §B closed Becker/B1 on a **reference-arm** argument: FAP
uninvolved mucosa was found GUCA2A-depleted relative to two healthy donors
(+1.689 log, 2.38× the control floor, n=2 healthy donors), and the argument was
that the germline *APC* hit makes the whole colon pre-lesional, so the paired
reference is contaminated by the phenomenon it references.

**The published data say the opposite, using assays that are not transcript
counting.**

- Blomain et al., *Cancer Biol Ther* 2020, "Silencing the intestinal GUCY2C
  tumor suppressor axis requires APC loss of heterozygosity"
  ([PMC7515455](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7515455)): guanylin
  protein **and** mRNA are *maintained* in normal epithelium under APC
  heterozygosity and lost only after LOH. In FAP patients, normal adjacent
  mucosa expressed guanylin while adjacent polyps and carcinoma did not. Mouse
  arm: expression quantitatively maintained in *Apc*^min/+ (monoallelic),
  eliminated in *Apc*^CKO tumours (biallelic). Assays: immunofluorescence,
  qRT-PCR, western, RNA-seq.
- The human FAP arm is **two patients**. Ours is **two healthy donors**. Neither
  side of this disagreement is powered; that is the honest statement.

**Why it matters.** If APC heterozygosity does not silence guanylin, then FAP
uninvolved mucosa is a *legitimate* reference arm and B1's second, "structural
to FAP" reason for closure does not hold. B1's *first* reason — four paired
donors — is untouched, and the gate that actually closed it
(`results/2026-09-07_2305f23/`, GUCA2A clearing the mature-cell enrichment floor
by depth rather than identity) is a separate finding and also untouched.

**Recommended handling:** do not reopen B1 on this. Record the disagreement in
`docs/NEXT_AVENUES.md` §B as an external, orthogonal-assay result that
contradicts the stated reason, so that the closure rests on the two reasons that
survive rather than on three of which one is contested.

### The same literature supplies a directional hypothesis for item 3

Bashir et al., *Hum Pathol* 2019, "Silencing the GUCA2A-GUCY2C tumor suppressor
axis in CIN, serrated, and MSI colorectal neoplasia"
([PubMed 30716341](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30716341/)): GUCA2A is
eliminated in tubular adenomas, serrated adenomas **and** MSI tumours, while the
GUCY2C receptor is retained in TA and MSI but **near-eliminated in serrated
lesions, attributed to loss of CDX2**.

So the field's prediction for the AD/SER split in §1 is specific and directional
and predates any of our data: **GUCA2A down in both arms, CDX2 down in the
serrated arm only.** That converts item 3 from an exploratory subgroup hunt into
a confirmatory test of a published prediction, which is a materially stronger
design — and it is consistent with §6d finding CDX2 indistinguishable from
housekeeping in a Chen cohort whose polyps are majority conventional adenoma.

Also relevant, and load-bearing for the paper's framing rather than for a run:
Blomain et al. 2020 (*Cancer Biol Ther*), "APC-β-catenin-TCF signaling silences
the intestinal guanylin-GUCY2C tumor suppressor axis" — the proposed mechanism
for GUCA2A loss is **transcriptional repression downstream of Wnt**, not
promoter methylation. That is the same direction as our own two negatives: the
pre-registered CIMP screen returned NOT SPECIFIC, and D1 found GUCA2A inside the
Wnt control floor.

## 3. An instrument positive control that is actually available

`docs/HANDOFF.md` §6g is the project's sharpest open wound: the coexpression
instrument's ability to detect a *known* silencing event has never been
demonstrated, MLH1 was the only candidate, and it cannot be run because the only
cohort with the ground truth is the one where the premise fails.

**The control does not have to be colorectal.** The question §6g poses is about
the instrument — *at this abundance and this depth, can a per-cell detection
statistic see a gene that is known to have been turned off in cells that are
still present?* That is a measurement-sensitivity question, and there is public
data built exactly for it.

**Replogle et al., *Cell* 2022 — genome-scale Perturb-seq** (`REPORTED`).
CRISPRi knockdown of ~2,000–9,900 genes in K562 and RPE1, ~310,000 cells at the
filtered scale, processed AnnData on Figshare+ and wrapped by `pertpy`
(`pertpy.data.replogle_2022_k562_gwps`).

Why it fits:

- The silencing is established by a **DNA-targeting perturbation**, not by
  transcription — the same independence MLH1 promoter methylation was chosen for.
- The cells are still there by construction, which is precisely the tumour-B
  condition the README's figure describes.
- Every targeted gene has its own measured on-target knockdown, so the control
  comes with a **dose ladder**, and baseline abundance varies across thousands of
  genes, so the deliverable is a *sensitivity curve* — detectable silencing as a
  function of baseline CP10K and sequencing depth — not a single pass/fail.
- Panel-relevant read-off: **CORRECTED 2026-09-10.** This read "GUCA2A sits at
  38.7 CP10K in the adenoma normal arm". **38.682 is the maximum over the 44
  patients, not where GUCA2A sits: the median is 5.476 and the mean 7.797**
  (`results/2026-09-06_765eb29/`, `lineage`, normal arm). MLH1's 0.039 is
  unchanged. The error matters for exactly the use it was written for —
  anchoring a sensitivity curve at 38.7 would have targeted a regime about
  seven times more favourable than the project's own, making the instrument
  look better than it is by construction. The corrected anchors are frozen in
  `prereg_replogle_sensitivity.md` §3. A curve says which of those regimes is
  inside the instrument's reach, which is the number §6g wanted and could not
  get.

**Honest limits, and the decisiveness is one-directional — the same asymmetry
§4a insists on for methylation, applied here.**

- **A negative is decisive.** If a gene at MLH1's abundance is undetectable in
  K562 at high depth and low dropout, it is undetectable in this project's
  substrates, and §6g's second branch fires on strictly better data than §6g
  could obtain.
- **A positive is not transferable as it stands**, because K562 and RPE1 sit at
  depths and dropout rates the tissue cohorts do not reach.
- **Two biases run in opposite directions and must not be stated as one.**
  CRISPRi knockdown is partial (typically 60–90%), and detecting a partial
  knockdown is *harder* than detecting complete silencing at the same abundance
  and depth — so that bias is **conservative** and makes a positive stronger
  than it looks, not weaker. The depth and dropout regime is
  **anti-conservative** and makes it weaker. They do not cancel in any
  quantified way.
- **The positive branch is repairable, and the repair is pre-specifiable.**
  Downsample the Perturb-seq counts to the median UMI and dropout of the target
  substrate (Pelka's 11,286 UMI; the Chen adenoma arm separately) and recompute
  the curve in the matched regime. That converts an optimistic bound into
  something closer to a calibration and costs only compute.

A sensitivity curve still cannot make the 13-study negative positive; it can
only say whether that negative had the power to be informative, which is what
"every null it has returned becomes evidence rather than absence of evidence"
requires.

**Gate:** pre-specify the abundance and depth grid, the detection statistic
(the same one `src/reference/detection_scale.py` implements), and the pass
criterion before touching the data. This is measurement infrastructure under
`CONTRIBUTING` §6, not a biological claim.

## 4. Orthogonal-modality substrates that exist and were not on the roadmap

### 4a. Adenoma methylation arrays — a locus-level test the CIMP screen never made

The pre-registered CIMP screen was **carcinoma** (TCGA), and it closed the
methylation leg. The project's actual finding is in **adenoma**, and no
methylation has ever been looked at there. Two public series, both laptop-sized:

| accession | content | `REPORTED` |
|---|---|---|
| `GSE288652` | EPIC v1 on **13 low-grade and 19 high-grade colorectal adenomas**, explicitly framed as a normal → LGA → HGA → carcinoma methylome trajectory, with non-tumour comparators drawn from GSE149282/GSE132804 | 32 samples, 2025-12 |
| `GSE233604` | Illumina 850k on colon polyps, from a high-risk tubular adenoma cohort (FFPE, FAP and Lynch **excluded** — sporadic by construction) | 66 samples, 2023-08 |

What it buys, and what it does not. If the GUCA2A promoter is **methylated** in
adenoma, intrinsic silencing has been observed by a non-transcript assay and
§6h gains an orthogonal corroboration. If it is **unmethylated** while the
per-cell transcript is down ~62%, that is consistent with the Wnt/TCF repression
mechanism above and inconsistent with locus methylation — informative, but *not*
decisive against composition, because a bulk promoter can be unmethylated simply
because the surviving stem-like cells never methylated it. **Only the positive
direction is decisive.** Say so in the pre-specification, not afterwards.

Composition confounding is the same problem as everywhere else and needs the
same honesty: a bulk methylation β is a mixture. A cell-type reference exists —
Loyfer et al., *Nature* 2023, "A DNA methylation atlas of normal human cell
types", 39 sorted cell types from 205 healthy samples including colon
epithelium, WGBS, with published fragment-level deconvolution — which is what
`docs/NEXT_AVENUES.md` Tier 2 costed at "weeks of new machinery". The reference
half of that machinery is now public. The atlas does **not** separate crypt-top
from crypt-bottom colonocytes, so it can supply an epithelial fraction and not a
maturity fraction. That limitation is fatal to using it as a compositional
estimator for this project's estimand and should be checked before, not after.

### 4b. A chromatin substrate on polyps that is not Becker

`GSE207954` (SuperSeries; subseries `GSE207949/207950/207951`, plus `GSE255300`)
— "Global decay of regulatory chromatin architecture promotes rebalancing of
gene expression during colorectal cancer development" (`REPORTED`). Described as
hundred-base-pair-resolution chromatin conformation across **33 colon tissues
tracing the formation and malignant transformation of colorectal polyps**, with
matched DNA methylation, chromatin accessibility and RNA on the same tissues.

This is axis 3's modality — identity and promoter state measured without
transcription — on polyps, from a deposit this repository has never touched. It
is **bulk**, so it cannot produce a per-cell intrinsic term and must not be
presented as replicating §6h. What it can do is ask whether the GUCA2A promoter
and its regulatory contacts remain accessible in adenoma, which is the mechanism
question the Wnt-repression hypothesis makes.

**Gate:** an `--inspect` before it is called an avenue, per §B's own rule — the
assay composition per tissue, whether polyps are sporadic, and whether normal is
patient-matched are all unverified here.

## 6. Context for the write-up, not for a run

- **Polyclonal origins of human premalignant colorectal lesions**, *Nature* 2026
  ([PMC12935544](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12935544)): 123
  samples from six FAP individuals; 40% of benign and 28% of dysplastic polyps
  are composed of several early-diverging genetic lineages. The authors'
  conclusion — that cell-intrinsic growth advantage alone does not explain
  initiation — is a caution for any lesion-level design that treats a polyp as
  one clone, including the AD/SER contrast in §1.
- **Guanylin is the field's canonical early loss.** Wilson et al., *Cancer
  Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev* 2014 ([PMC4221461](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4221461)):
  guanylin mRNA loss confirmed at protein level by IHC, "eliminated in 100% of
  tumours examined". Human Protein Atlas carries GUCA2A at 670.9 nTPM in colon
  with antibody HPA018215 and IHC in normal colon and colorectal cancer — a
  cell-resolved protein readout, though **not on adenoma**, so it cannot serve
  §6h.
- The premise that differentiation failure *precedes* cancer is independently
  argued in `GSE224679`/`GSE221299` ("impaired differentiation is a conserved
  event preceding cancer development"), in mouse models plus one hereditary
  polyposis patient.

## 7. What I would actually do with this, in order

1. **Record §2 in `NEXT_AVENUES.md` §B.** Costs nothing, and a closure that
   rests on a contested reason is the kind of defect §3 of `HANDOFF.md` is about.
2. **Size the pathology-labelled subgroup properly and decide item 3 on it**
   (§1). The public join is done and the numbers are above; what is missing is
   the terms review, the provenance confirmation, the multi-lesion patient rule
   and a pre-committed precision rule. This is the cheapest real gain available.
3. **Keep Synapse certification alive for the compositional term only** (§1a).
   Pathology labels are confounded with the compositional endpoint and genotype
   labels are not, so certification buys the one contrast pathology cannot give
   — descriptively, at 5 versus 4 patients, not as a test. Do not chase it for
   the intrinsic term, where 13 versus 9 already wins.
4. **The Perturb-seq sensitivity curve** (§3). It is the only route found to the
   §6g control, it is measurement infrastructure rather than biology, and it
   changes how every existing null in this project may be described.
5. **The adenoma methylation check** (§4a), with the asymmetry of its two
   outcomes written into the pre-specification.
6. `--inspect` on `GSE207954` (§4b); metadata-only inventory of HTAN Phase 2
   colon (§5). Both are gates, neither is an avenue yet.

**None of this outranks the write-up.** The WMHS deadline is 15 September 2026
and roughly fifteen results from 2026-09-06/07 are in neither paper.

## 8. Corrections, 2026-09-10, after review of the first draft

Recorded rather than silently edited, because a list of findings whose errors
are invisible is the defect this repository keeps writing about.

1. **"61 samples / 26 patients" was wrong; it is 61 samples / 52 patients.**
   The 26 was read from a summarised API response, and 26 is the study's **H&E
   slide resource count**, not its patient count. Verified against
   `/api/studies/crc_hta11_htan_2021` (`allSampleCount: 61`) and
   `/api/studies/crc_hta11_htan_2021/patients` (52), and against 52 distinct
   `patientId` in the clinical table. The error was internally impossible — the
   doc claimed 31 labelled patients inside a 26-patient study — which is how it
   was caught. **Never quote a summarised API response; quote the JSON.**
2. **`ADVANCED` missingness was undeclared, and is worse than first reported.**
   §1 now carries the completeness tables. It is 12 of 35 uninformative (34%),
   not 9: `Not Stated` and `Not stated` are two spellings of one category, and
   three biospecimens carry no attribute at all. The missingness is not random —
   5 of 11 `SER` against 1 of 16 `AD`.
3. **"On the same footing as the WES label" was too strong**, and §1a is the
   correction. Invariant 11 tests label provenance, not whether two modalities
   measure the same tissue property; a morphology label is confounded with the
   *compositional* endpoint and clean against the *intrinsic* one. The
   recommendation to drop Synapse certification was rewritten accordingly: it is
   worth keeping for the compositional term, descriptively.
4. **The patient-level arms were overstated by one each.** "14 versus 10"
   double-counted `HTA11_6801`, which carries one `AD` and one `SER` lesion.
   Under invariant 5 the arms are **13 versus 9** with that patient excluded.
5. **Replogle's decisiveness asymmetry is now stated explicitly**, as §4a's is,
   together with the correction that partial knockdown biases *conservatively*
   and only the depth regime biases the other way — and with the downsampling
   repair that makes the positive branch usable.

Items 1–3 and 5 were raised in review; item 4 was found while sizing the
pre-specification and is a further error in the first draft, not a review point.
