## Page budget

**It fits, and only just.** `neurips_2026.sty` is not in this repository, so the
paper has never been compiled against the real style. Compiled against the
published NeurIPS geometry, the main text fills **page 8** to the bottom and the
References heading sits at the **top of page 9**. There is no margin left:
anything added needs something cut first.

**Measure the spill, not the heading.** While adding § 3's rare-label
paragraph the main text ran ~81 words onto page 9 and a checker that looked for
the page containing "References" still reported eight pages — the heading had
moved *down* page 9, not onto page 10. `build.sh` catches this (it measures how
far into the page the heading sits); a naive check does not. That is this paper's
own subject, committed by its own tooling.

Three cuts got it here and are applied: Figure 2 (`fig_tiers`) removed, nothing
referenced it and §4 states its numbers; Table 2's `width gate` row removed, its
numbers moved to the prose; §5 cut to a single paragraph. Then, absorbing the
review response, three figure captions were cut back to what a caption is for —
they had been restating their sections — and §8's two abstention rules merged
into one.

A later pass paid for §3's rare-label paragraph by merging two §3 paragraphs into
one, tightening the normalisation paragraph, and compressing the
withdrawn-number paragraph in §2. Two sentences went in that pass because the new
evidence had made them **false**, not to save space: *"what we cannot claim is
that we caught it biting"* and *"that is the theorem talking, not this cohort"*.

## Build

```
./build.sh              # builds and FAILS if the main text exceeds 8 pages
./check_anonymity.sh    # double-blind guard; must exit 0 before submitting
python paper/icbinb/make_figs.py    # regenerates Figures 1 and 2 from results/
```

Both guards now **fail when they cannot run**, rather than reporting PASS. That
was not true before: `build.sh` skipped the page check and passed when `pypdf`
was missing, and `check_anonymity.sh` reported "safe to submit" while skipping
the PDF text and metadata scans because no PDF had been built. Set
`SKIP_PAGE_CHECK=1` to say deliberately that you are not checking the limit.
`pdfinfo` (poppler) is needed for the metadata scan.

`neurips_2026.sty` is not vendored — download the official style and put it
beside `main.tex`.

## Figures

All four are drawn at **the width they are printed at** (`\linewidth` = 5.5in)
and included at that width, so a 7.5pt tick label is 7.5pt on the page. They were
previously drawn on a 9.2in canvas and included at 0.80\linewidth, a 0.48x
shrink that put every axis label at 3–4pt — legible on screen, not on paper.
If you change a figure's size, change `figsize` and the `\includegraphics` width
together or the point sizes stop meaning anything.

`figures/fig1_calibration.pdf` and `figures/fig3_generator_statistic.pdf` come
from `../wmhs`, generated by its `make_fig1.py` / `make_fig3.py` off the same
committed tables. Regenerate there and copy across; do not edit either in place.

## Known gaps

**Closed 2026-09-01** — see [PATCHES.md](PATCHES.md) for the full list and the
evidence behind each.

- `(0.36, 0.31)` → `(0.72, 0.62)` in §7: the numbers are gone; §7 states the
  mechanism instead.
- The two repairs the paper described in the past tense are now real.
  `src/harness/depth_confound.py` pairs each arm's correlation with **that arm's**
  bound and names arms whose correlation is undefined instead of dropping them;
  `src/harness/calibration.py` counts abstentions in `n_abstained` and gives an
  all-abstained bin the verdict `all_abstained`. Both have failing-input
  regression tests in `tests/test_checks_can_fail.py`.
- `_tables.py` resolves "newest" by the sidecar's `utc_timestamp`, not
  lexicographically. It was picking the 17:02 run over the 20:45 one.
- §3's `66 of 190`, `3.9x` and `21 of 166` are gone — they needed per-arm rho on
  GSE178341 and no committed table carries it. `64 of 64 → 16` stays; it follows
  from prevalence alone.
- §4's tier-B abundances quoted two different reads at once and one number
  (`19.4`) that is in neither. Now one stratum from one read, with a floor
  (`never below 200-fold`) that holds on all four.
