\section{Calibration details and limits}
\label{app:gridcaveats}

\paragraph{Historical grids.}\label{app:historical}
Before the controlled run, the SMC reference-only sweeps returned binned joint
candidates of 100, 90 and 70 on the committed, filled and dense grids, with
500, 500 and 200 replicates per setting, respectively. Their adjacent crossing
brackets were $(40,100]$, $(65,90]$ and $(42.5,70]$, describing evaluated bin
summaries rather than confidence intervals. The 100-to-90 change occurred
within the same bin, $(76,107]$: adding counts 80 and 90 to 100 changed both
its median count and its aggregated outcomes. Random streams and bin boundaries
also differed, so this comparison alone did not isolate a resolution effect.
The controlled analysis in Section~\ref{sec:calibration} supersedes it for that
question. Under pooled draws no joint cutpoint qualified in either cohort's
historical grids. KUL3 reference-only candidates were 400 or 800; its largest
setting sampled 800 mature cells per arm with replacement from 844 distinct
mature source cells. Sampling with replacement is a valid empirical-pool
simulation, but an 800-draw arm is not 800 distinct cells or additional
independent patients.

\paragraph{Recovery accounting and unavailable contrasts.}\label{app:generator}
The four diagnostic sweeps comprise 1{,}711{,}200 replicates: 1{,}283{,}400 with
nonzero requested intrinsic reference and 427{,}800 with zero requested
reference, for which ratios are undefined. The first 50-replicate sweep has
119{,}600 draws, partitioned into 89{,}700 and 29{,}900, respectively. The
maximum estimate--realised-reference residual is zero throughout. At a
zero-cell setting the raw ratio is $1/(1-s)$ when the denominator is nonzero.
These raw diagnostic values arise from the harness setting an empty-arm mean
to zero; they do not define an effect in absent cells. The reporting gate
abstains and the interval is undefined. Separately, undefined intervals were
previously counted as failures through comparisons with \texttt{NaN}; corrected
rates exclude them and report abstentions. An all-abstaining bin has no
coverage estimate. One historical seed gives $ok=wide=45$, showing that a
three-state specification need not yield three distinct fitted regions.

\paragraph{Biological scope and patient uncertainty.}\label{app:guards}
\label{app:modal}\label{app:limitations}
The finest annotation resolution yields abstention in 28/28 patients under one
population definition and 24/28 under the other; coarser definitions do not
resolve identifiability. The coarsest definition labels every epithelial cell
mature and forces the compositional term to zero. In a separate comparability
analysis over three cohorts and 42 patients, 83 of 84 housekeeping-detection
control rows pass a 0.10 tolerance despite expression changes of 1.19--1.64
fold. Patient-bootstrap log$_2$ expression intervals cross the accepted
$[-0.5,0.5]$ boundaries in every cohort, leaving the comparisons undecided.
Depth matching discards roughly three quarters of the cells and pushes about
half the remaining estimable rows below the count threshold. The two source
pools also differ jointly in tissue mixture, dispersion and distinct mature-cell
counts (662 versus 3{,}824); their comparison cannot isolate these factors.
These observations restrict biological interpretation. Repeated cell draws and
overlapping patient pairs do not supply independent patient replication.

\section{Supporting trial and generator checks}
\label{app:matrix}

\paragraph{Reference dependence beyond the main comparison.}
For logistic-propensity IPW, confounded-design maximum residuals against the
standardised reference are 0.403, 0.045, 0.0103 and 0.010 at
$C=0.1,1,100,10^6$; tightening solver tolerance to $10^{-8}$ reduces the last
to $1.3\times10^{-6}$. Under increasing effect heterogeneity ($\tau=0$ to 16,
fixed population ATE), OLS recovery grows from 1.003 to 1.132, while its residual
against its own variance-weighted reference stays at numerical precision.
Recovery can therefore reveal target mismatch even when functional equality
persists. These results supplement Table~\ref{tab:matrix}.

\paragraph{Residual scale.}\label{app:scale}
The optional descriptor
$\rho=\mathrm{med}|\hat\theta-T|/\mathrm{med}|T-\theta|$ compares typical
departure with reference error when its denominator is positive. It is zero
at exact equality and undefined when the denominator is zero. Because the two
terms in $\hat\theta-\theta=(\hat\theta-T)+(T-\theta)$ can covary or cancel,
$\rho$ neither decomposes total error nor ranks estimators. A zero median can
also conceal nonzero residuals, so it cannot replace the maximum-residual check.

\paragraph{Independent Cox implementation check.}\label{app:cox}
The survival sweep uses 1{,}200 paired draws per setting; at 78\% censoring
and 100 patients, 463 have nonfinite fits and are excluded. Eight stratified and unadjusted Cox fits on one fixed 1{,}500-patient cohort at
four censoring horizons agree with \texttt{lifelines.CoxPHFitter} to
$1.4\times10^{-7}$, below the specified $10^{-5}$ tolerance. All eight pairs
are finite. This checks the implemented coefficients on that cohort, not
coverage or convergence over the simulation grid.

\paragraph{Learned-generator design and exclusions.}\label{app:learned}
The pre-registration preceded the module; a recorded amendment added penalised
outcome models after the original estimator set could not test the proposed
near-equality interval. Seven generators (mixtures at $K=1,2,4,8$, an MLP
conditional Gaussian and two parametric controls), ten estimators, six cohort
sizes, six seed streams and 30 replicates schedule 75{,}600 evaluations on
7{,}560 synthetic cohorts (seed 20260913). After generator failures the saved
table contains 75{,}580 evaluations on 7{,}558 cohorts. Against the drawn
reference, 74{,}726 pairs are finite and 854 are excluded: 410 have nonfinite
references and 731 nonfinite estimates, overlapping in 287 evaluations. All
420 generator-by-estimator-by-size cells retain valid pairs. Table~\ref{tab:learned}
uses maxima within each generator family, conditional on those pairs.

\paragraph{Tolerance and the model-based target.}
NumPy's default comparisons use $10^{-8}$ against a zero residual and
$10^{-8}+10^{-5}|T|$ for estimate versus reference. Thirteen generator--estimator combinations
fail both at $n=100$ but pass only the second at $n=5{,}000$; all use ridge
penalties. In the saturated ridge model, fitted means are
$\bar y_{ga}n_{ga}/(n_{ga}+\alpha)$, so a fixed penalty diminishes with cell
count. This numerical transition does not imply declining statistical validity.
The fitted generator's population effect $T_{\mathrm{model}}$ is a different
reference from the empirical $T(\tilde D)$. Mixture references use 200{,}000
Monte Carlo observations; saved median standard errors are 0.019--0.027.
Parametric and MLP references are analytic. In
$\hat\theta-\theta=(\hat\theta-T_{\mathrm{model}})+(T_{\mathrm{model}}-\theta)$,
estimator departure and generator target discrepancy can covary. The finite
Monte Carlo budget limits numerical resolution, not the target's intrinsic
precision; neither reference validates a model for real patients.

\section{The external-control demonstration}
\label{app:eca}

Pre-registered in \texttt{docs/prereg\_external\_control\_demo.md} with nine
falsifiers, committed before the module. None fired. Registry pool of fixed
size; enrolment without replacement under eligibility weights enriching for
refractory patients; an effect-modified benefit induced on each record's own
outcome; external control arm three times the trial; six cohort sizes, six
seeds, seed 20260915.

The middle row is the one that corrected our own prescription. Its residual is
not zero, so a rule of ``choose a reference the estimator does not reproduce''
is satisfied --- and it still ranks the defective estimator first, because it
names the wrong population. Breaking the equality is necessary and not
sufficient.

Balance diagnostics do not catch it either. Under the defective weighting the
worst between-arm standardised mean difference is $5.6\times10^{-16}$, while
the same weighted pseudo-population differs from the enrolled patients by
$0.964$. Under the correct weighting both are at machine zero. Protocols
tabulate the first column.

Three honest limits. The workflow is constructed rather than observed, so it
bounds harm and not prevalence. The defective estimator's recovery ratio is
exactly $1$ with zero interquartile range rather than tightening with $n$,
which is itself a signal a careful reader might notice. And the scenario rests
on the analyst misidentifying the target population in both the estimator and
the reference; we argue that is one belief expressed twice rather than two
independent errors, but a reader who rejects that rejects the demonstration.

\section{External audit and research context}
\label{app:prevalence}

\paragraph{Search and classification.}
A pre-registered queue contained 41 entries; 19 were screened out and seven
classified within the 26 entries examined in order. An amendment before code
inspection relaxed unsuccessful queries; the classification rule was unchanged.
Generating-parameter references or quantities computed outside the estimator's
observed records give \textsc{no}. Same-record references require examination
of inputs, weights, conditioning, nuisance fitting and observation process;
\textsc{yes} requires affirmative evidence of functional identity. All seven
recorded verdicts are \textsc{no}: \texttt{causl}, target-trial-emulation,
\texttt{ecasim}, PlasmodeSimulation, StatsPAI, \texttt{diff-diff}, and
Auto-Empirical-Research-Skills. The first six use generating parameters or
outside-sample quantities; the last compares cross-fitted partialling-out with
full-sample OLS. Its $4.0\times10^{-14}$ residual on noiseless linear data
becomes 0.125 after adding outcome noise. The functions agree on the restricted
noiseless support but not on general noisy datasets; the \textsc{no} verdict
concerns shared functional form. Classification used one reader, only this last
repository was executed, and the sample is nonrandom. The virtual-control-arm
query located no examinable code. These limits preclude a prevalence estimate.

\paragraph{Related scope.}\label{app:relatedwork}
Cell-count filters are established in pseudobulk analysis \citep{crowell2020};
scCODA, Milo, propeller and DA-seq address composition
\citep{buettner2021,dann2022,phipson2022,zhao2021}, while cacoa separates
composition and expression changes \citep{petukhov2022}. Selective prediction
motivates evaluating both answers and abstentions
\citep{chow1970,elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. Conformal prediction coverage
\citep{vovk2005,angelopoulos2023} does not define a missing conditional
population or automatically provide intervals for this intrinsic parameter.

\paragraph{Documentary source of the case.}\label{app:provenance}
The quoted validation claim and its six-draw recovery table occur in
\texttt{docs/gate\_memo\_w2.md}, Section 15. The interval requirements occur in
\texttt{docs/harness\_design\_spec.md}, Section 4. The former declares the
50-cell cutpoint validated; the latter requires 90\% inclusion and 80\%
exclusion of zero at $s=0.5$. Section~\ref{sec:calibration} reproduces the
relevant claim and numbers so this comparison can be inspected without access
to an identified repository. These are internal documents, not a published
clinical validation. Checksums and exact source excerpts accompany the revision.

\paragraph{Implementation and provenance.}\label{app:withdrawn}
Corrections separated population definitions collapsed by de-duplication,
derived fractions and means from one labelling call, tracked producing scripts,
and repaired undefined-interval denominators. Saved tables retain commit hashes
and seeds. These implementation checks support traceability, not statistical
validity; the draw-dependent calibration target is stated in
Equation~\ref{eq:requested}.
