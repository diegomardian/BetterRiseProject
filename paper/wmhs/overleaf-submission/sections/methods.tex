\section{Simulation settings}
\label{app:methods}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.19\linewidth}p{0.76\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Experiment} & \textbf{Fixed settings and sampling units} \\
\midrule
Single-cell calibration &
GSE132465 (SMC): 10 paired patients; GSE144735 (KUL3): 6.
Target: GUCA2A; \texttt{stem\_pole} axis, \texttt{lineage} resolution,
\texttt{differentiated} mature label; reference (\texttt{normal}) weighting.
Each replicate holds out two patients and draws 2{,}000 cells per synthetic
arm from their eligible pool; reference mature fraction $0.40$.
Expression shifts are $1.0,0.8,0.5,0.25$; calibration uses $0.5$. \\
Interval construction &
A nominal 95\% percentile interval from 200 resamples of mature cells
within each synthetic arm, holding cell fractions fixed. This assesses the
conditional intrinsic contrast; it does not estimate population uncertainty
across patients. Coverage and discrimination condition on defined intervals. Coverage uses
$f_N(s-1)\bar Y_N$ with the drawn reference-arm mean, not a fixed pool mean. \\
Patient outcomes &
Stratum probabilities $(0.5,0.3,0.2)$, baseline means $(10,14,25)$ and
$Y\mid(g,A)\sim\mathcal N(\mu_g+3A,4^2)$.
Confounded treatment probabilities $(0.25,0.50,0.75)$; alternatives are
independent Bernoulli assignment at $0.5$ and complete balanced blocks of size 4
within strata. The three-design sweep uses 200 replicates per size and seed
across six seeds. \\
Survival outcomes &
The same stratum and confounded-assignment probabilities; exponential event
rate $\lambda_g\exp(\beta A)$, $\lambda=(0.10,0.16,0.30)$, $\exp(\beta)=0.6$.
Administrative horizons $1000,20.065216,6.072920,2.028858$ target
$0\%,12\%,49\%,78\%$ censoring. The same latent cohort is viewed through
each horizon; RMST uses $\tau=2$. Each size has 200 replicates per seed over
six seeds. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Settings are recorded by \texttt{calibration\_gap}, \texttt{trial\_recovery}
and \texttt{trial\_survival}; the dense calibration grid is specified by
\texttt{cutpoint\_dense\_grid}. Historical grid budgets are reported in
Appendix~\ref{app:historical}; Section~\ref{sec:calibration} specifies the
controlled runs. These are existing simulations, not clinical-trial data.
