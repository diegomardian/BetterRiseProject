\section{What the blind curve hid}
\label{sec:calibration}

Three acceptance criteria were fixed in a design document before any sweep ran:
a detectable halving of per-cell output, $s = 0.5$; interval coverage of 90\%;
and discrimination of 80\%, the probability the interval excludes zero at that
effect.\iffull{} The cutpoints follow mechanically --- the smallest $n$ meeting
both criteria, and the smallest meeting coverage alone.\fi The pre-registration is
mechanical rather than documentary: the panel, the labelling axes and the
falsification rule are frozen in a configuration file

\emph{both} tissues pooled.

\paragraph{The grid was reporting its own resolution.} Our committed grid varies
a mature \emph{fraction} against a fixed 2{,}000 cells, so the counts it can
reach are $\{0, 20, 40, 100, 200, 400, 800\}$ --- nothing between 40 and 100. On
the reference pool it returns $ok = 100$ on every one of the eight seeds at 500
replicates\iffull{}, and on twelve of thirteen at 50,\fi{} and that stability is
what made the number look like a measurement.

\iffull

\textbf{And the reported cutpoint is not a count. It is a bin label.} Counts are
binned into twelve log-spaced intervals before coverage and discrimination are
computed, and the value reported for a bin is the \emph{median of the counts
that landed in it}. Over $20$--$800$ the edges are $\{0, 20, 28, 39, 55, 76,
107, 150, 209, 293, 409, 572, 800\}$, and they are identical for every grid
because they are derived from the same span. Nothing in the output says so, and
we did not know it while writing the paragraph above.

We added nine fractions inside the gap, reaching 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 110, 120,
130 and 150, and changed nothing else. The answer moves to 90 --- and it moves
\emph{within one bin}. Both grids return the interval $(76, 107]$; the committed
grid held only $\{100\}$ there and the filled grid holds $\{80, 90, 100\}$, so
the median fell from 100 to 90 while the bin boundaries stayed put; the added counts change the evidence
aggregated within that bin. \textbf{At the resolution of the statistic that produced it, the answer did
not move at all.} What the filled grid did buy is a genuinely new bin at
$(55, 76]$, where discrimination reads $0.789$ --- under target, so the crossing
lies above it.

\textbf{A third grid, and the number keeps falling.} A denser grid reaching
below 20 --- attainable counts $\{2.5, 10, 17.5, 27.5, 42.5, 70, 105, 150, 400,
800\}$ --- returns $ok = 70$ on eight of eight seeds. Discrimination against
count is smooth, and the three grids agree on it wherever they overlap
(medians over eight seeds of $0.712$, $0.728$ and $0.726$ near 40, and $0.880$,
$0.858$ and $0.878$ near 100). What
differs is only which point each grid happens to carry first above the $0.80$
line:

\textbf{The reported cutpoint decreases across these tested grids; this does not
establish monotonicity or non-convergence under further refinement. No tested
grid, cohort or seed returned the committed 50.} Each value is the first point above a crossing, reported as
though it were the crossing. Within any one grid $wide$ has \emph{exactly zero}
seed-to-seed variance --- one value on all eight seeds, in all six
cohort-by-grid cells we ran ($40$, $45$, $42$ here; $100$, $65$, $70$ on the
second cohort) --- so the quantity that looks most like a measurement is the one
whose stability comes entirely from having nowhere else to land. The pooled draw
returns no cutpoint under any of the three grids, on any seed of either cohort,
discrimination topping out at $0.750$ and $0.795$ against the $0.80$ target, so
the non-identifiability there is not a resolution artefact.

\textbf{We do not endorse a calibrated cutpoint: the procedure returns
grid-dependent candidates without patient-level uncertainty.} It returns its own grid, and it did so with a stability that
survived three grids, thirteen seeds and two cohorts without once warning us.
On the real cohort, rather than on the sweep, abstention is the modal outcome
under both population definitions at the resolution the compositional question
is defined on, and the coarse resolutions return \emph{not identifiable}
rather than a number.
\fi

\iffull
A second cohort agrees on the verdict and not the number: its pooled draw
returns no cutpoint on any seed, and its reference draw returns $400$ or
$800$, the top two counts its grid can express. Eight to sixteen times the
committed 50, by the same mechanism.

\paragraph{Three further statistics, and where they are.} The same failure
recurs three more times in this pipeline --- a confound diagnostic whose
tolerance sat above its own attainable bound, a pre-registered criterion that
could not pass when the hypothesis held, and a premise check whose sensitivity
was a property of the gene it controlled. Each survives correct code and each
is recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:guards} rather than argued here; none of this
paper's claims rests on them. The fourth is not a guard at all.
