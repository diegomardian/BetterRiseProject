\section{Use of large language models}
\label{app:llm}
A language-model coding assistant, working under author direction, assisted with
analysis code, figures and manuscript drafting and editing. Independently
prompted model audits also helped identify defects; assistant-written code
introduced some of the defects described in Appendix~\ref{app:withdrawn}.
Authors remain responsible for the analysis and claims. Numerical results are
traced to versioned tables carrying a commit hash and fixed seed. Automated
checks re-derive selected results, but do not verify every statement in the
manuscript; the full paper's editorial revision also requires review against
those tables.
