\section{Use of large language models}
\label{app:llm}
Language-model assistants supported coding, figures, drafting and independent
model audits under author direction. They helped identify defects and introduced
some of those in Appendix~\ref{app:withdrawn}. Authors remain responsible for
all claims. Current simulation results have versioned tables, commit hashes
and fixed seeds. Automated checks
re-derive selected results and complement author review, without verifying
every manuscript statement.
