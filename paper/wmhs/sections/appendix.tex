\subsection*{Related work}
Cell-count filters of this shape are standard rather than novel. Pseudobulk
pipelines drop samples below a minimum cell count \citep{crowell2020}, and
differential-abundance methods --- scCODA \citep{buettner2021}, Milo
\citep{dann2022}, propeller \citep{phipson2022}, DA-seq \citep{zhao2021} ---
test composition without an expression arm, so they never face this estimand.
cacoa \citep{petukhov2022} is closest: it separates compositional from
expression shifts explicitly.

\section{The withdrawn guards}
\label{app:withdrawn}

Three defects, recorded because each one survived ordinary review and because
the first two are this paper's argument in a third and fourth instance: a
statistic computed over a population nobody chose, and a check that could not
fail. Two further entries --- a leakage guard checked against a symbol list that
could not contain the identifiers under comparison, and an acceptance test
comparing gene symbols against Ensembl identifiers over an empty intersection ---
were ordinary implementation errors with nothing to learn from, and are named
here rather than described.

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep3pt
\item \textbf{A de-duplication that collapsed two population definitions.} A
per-patient de-duplication took the first row per patient from a frame carrying
\emph{two} pre-registered definitions of the diseased arm, selecting one by file
order --- the same one in 224 of 224 combinations, and neither the table, its
sidecar nor the internal report recorded which. Re-run per definition, the
finding at the finest resolution survives under both and the coarse resolutions
become \emph{not identifiable}, which is a stronger statement than the
indeterminacy originally reported.

\item \textbf{A join that validated its keys and never its counts.} We assembled
the primary decomposition table by joining per-patient \emph{means} from one job
onto per-patient \emph{fractions} and cell counts from another. The two jobs
label cells at different quality-control thresholds, 0.10 and 0.25 of the depth
distribution, so the mature-cell populations differ by a median factor of 1.26 on
the reference arm and 1.35 on the comparison arm. Every term in
Equation~\ref{eq:kitagawa} multiplied a fraction over one set of cells by a mean
over another. The count the abstention rule reads came from the first labelling,
the mean it gated from the second. The producing job's documentation asserts a
loop mirroring the other pipeline exactly and enumerates the filters. The
threshold is the single filter the loop does not mirror. The join written ``so
the two cannot drift'' validates join keys and never the counts each side
implies. The replacement job derives fractions, means, counts and estimability
from a single labelling call per patient, then asserts its counts against the
committed counting function. That assertion is the one line missing before.

\item \textbf{A provenance stamp blind to uncommitted code.} Every result must
carry a commit hash and a fixed seed, and the writer refuses a table lacking
either --- but the dirtiness check ignored untracked files, so a table produced
by a script that was never committed recorded a \emph{clean} hash pointing at a
commit that did not contain the script. Three results reached an internal report
this way, one of them the calibration behind this paper's central figure.
Re-deriving it also surfaced the replicate count: that run used 6 per grid point
against the design document's 50 to 100. The check now counts untracked files
and the sidecar names them.
\end{enumerate}

Items 1 and 2 are one failure twice over. Someone trying to prevent exactly that
failure wrote both; neither felt wrong, and both survived ordinary review. Item 3
is why \S\ref{sec:calibration}'s numbers are re-derived here from a committed,
seeded table rather than quoted from the report that first carried them --- and
it is the one defect in this paper that is now closed, with the sweep committed
and the check tested against the input it used to pass.

\paragraph{What is and is not fixed.} Three guards carry regression tests that
fail against the version they replace. The width gate's finiteness guard
(\S\ref{sec:bench}) fails against the version comparing before checking; the
attainable-bound identity (\S\ref{sec:calibration}) is tested at four
prevalences; and the provenance check in item~3 now counts untracked files and
names them in the sidecar, against a fixture repository holding an uncommitted
producing script --- the input that made the old check return clean. The
calibration sweep is committed with it, so the number and the code that produced
it now travel together. \textbf{The two defects in
\S\ref{sec:calibration} do not yet carry regression tests, and the code that
produced them is unchanged.} The undefined-interval coercion still returns
\texttt{False} on a null interval and still lets an abstention score as a
failure; the independent-maxima pairing still takes the worst correlation and
the largest bound over different arms. We describe both as live rather than
repaired, because a paper reporting that checks are hard to get right should not
claim fixes it has not landed.

\section{The curve itself}
\label{app:generator}

\S\ref{sec:blind} states the general form and the one-line check. This appendix
carries the curve it was read off, and two details that belong with it rather
than in the argument.

The sweep is the calibration one, run by
\texttt{src.harness.calibration\_gap}. Its result table carries a
\texttt{max\_abs\_residual\_vs\_realised} column, which is the one-line check
persisted rather than re-derived: it is identically zero exactly when the curve
cannot see the estimator. It is zero on every row.

The second detail is where the statistic is not merely uninformative but
undefined. At $s = 1$ --- the design's own null control, the arm that exists to
confirm the estimator recovers zero when nothing happened --- the parametric
intrinsic term is exactly $0$, so the ratio divides by zero. 29{,}900 of the
sweep's rows carry no value at all. The one point the design included to check
the statistic is the one point at which the statistic cannot be evaluated.

\section{The competitor benchmark}
\label{app:bench}

Six worlds, 200 replicates, 2{,}000 cells per arm, seed 20260829. Mature cells
express $\mathrm{Poisson}(\mu)$ in reference tissue and $\mathrm{Poisson}(\mu s)$
in diseased tissue; immature cells express nothing. The arm mean is
$f \times$ (mean among mature), exactly what Equation~\ref{eq:kitagawa} splits,
so the truth is available in closed form rather than by simulation. The world
that matters sets the diseased mature fraction to zero: no diseased mature cell
survives and the intrinsic estimand has no referent.

Six methods, of which five run. \texttt{cacoa} ships as an adapter that reports
its own unavailability rather than being silently absent --- it installs from
GitHub and is not in the pinned environment --- so it appears as a named skip
with a reason.

\paragraph{The width gate.} Abstain when the 95\% half-width on the intrinsic
term exceeds the detectable effect, both taken from the same design document as
the count cutpoint, so neither gate is tuned against the other here. The
per-cell moments are recovered from the full arm without needing the mature-cell
mask, because immature cells express exactly zero and contribute nothing to
either the sum or the sum of squares. \textbf{Finiteness is checked before the
comparison.} Written the obvious way --- comparing first --- the gate returns
\texttt{False} on an undefined standard error and declines to abstain in exactly
the world that motivates it.

\paragraph{Limitations.} Everything here is synthetic, and the generative model
is exactly the one Equation~\ref{eq:kitagawa} assumes, which is a favourable
setting. It tests the gate, not robustness to a misspecified model. The
competitors are faithful reimplementations, not the published software. The
naive difference of arm means and pseudobulk DE answer a slightly different
question, so their bias is partly unfair to them; the false-confidence result
does not depend on them and the ablation does not depend on them at all. That
the width criterion never binds is a statement about these regimes: a halving of
per-cell output is large relative to sampling error at 2{,}000 cells per arm, and
a smaller detectable effect or a heavier-tailed expression model would move the
binding point. What survives regardless is that the finiteness guard, not the
threshold, catches the undefined estimand.

\section{Extended limitations}
\label{app:limitations}

\paragraph{Keeping the third verdict distinct.} The third verdict is a value, not
a flag, and the results writer refuses a row carrying a number where none
belongs. The guard is necessary and insufficient.
\S\ref{sec:calibration}\emph{(ii)} shows the same coercion two layers up as a
boolean, inside a rate whose denominator the writer never sees. We report the
interaction term separately rather than folding it into either arm. Anyone
reusing the decomposition should keep all three properties. The method is not
wrong. Its output flattens easily.

\paragraph{The calibration's own rates carry no interval, and the replicate
count does not buy what it looks like it buys.} Coverage and discrimination are
reported as bare point estimates, though the project's other
proportion-against-a-line carries a Wilson interval and a \emph{resolvable} flag
for exactly this reason. At 50 replicates the crossing does not resolve: the
returned cutpoint wanders between 45 and 90 with the seed. At 500 it does, with
7 of 8 seeds returning $ok = 90$ --- \textbf{but this stabilises Monte Carlo
noise only, and not the quantity a reader will take it for.} Ten patients with
two held out per replicate admit just 45 distinct holdout pairs, so at 500
replicates each pair is drawn roughly eleven times and the estimate converges to
the value over those 45 pairs rather than to a population value. The
patient-level uncertainty is unchanged by the replicate count and is not
quantified anywhere in this paper. A reader should treat $ok = 90$ as what this
procedure returns on this cohort, not as a cutpoint with an interval on it. That
is the same error the paper documents twice elsewhere --- a statistic reporting
more precision than its inputs support --- and we flag it here rather than let a
larger replicate count imply otherwise.

\paragraph{The two draw pools differ in more than one way at once.} Neither
matches the population the estimator meets in real data. There the reference arm
holds reference-tissue cells and the comparison arm does not, whereas the second
reading puts reference-tissue cells into \emph{both} arms. A generator drawing
each arm from its own tissue is a third option we did not implement, because the
imposed shift would then sit on top of a real between-tissue difference and the
truth would stop being known. The two pools also differ in target-gene
dispersion, in how many distinct cells a replicate draws from (662 mature cells
against 3{,}824, sampled with replacement), and in whether the mature-cell
distribution is unimodal or a mixture of two tissues. The sweep varies all three
together and identifies none. A dispersion-only account predicts discrimination
rising with $\sqrt{n}$, and neither curve does. Per-pool descriptive statistics
travel in the result sidecar, with their scope named in the field names: all
patients, before the shift, not the cells a replicate draws.

\paragraph{Depth matching is expensive.} The matching removing the
absence-defined axis's depth mechanism discards roughly three quarters of the
cells and pushes about half the remaining estimable rows below the positivity
cutpoint. On that cohort you get a clean comparison or an estimable one. The
trade belongs to the correction rather than to the cohort size, and the
abstention rule is what makes it visible.

\paragraph{Estimation runs per study, and the meta-analysis does not exist.}
Batch correction removes between-dataset variation, which is where the
compositional signal lives, so estimates run per study rather than pooled. Our
design named meta-analytic intervals as an output and the data cannot supply
them. A meta-analysis needs two quotable (study, rung) cells and this project
has one, the primary cohort's lineage rung on the depth-matched read. At
$k = 1$ an engine combining one study returns a number looking meta-analytic
without being one.

\paragraph{The unit of inference is the patient.} All bootstrapping resamples
patients, never cells. An earlier internal result violated this and reported a
cell-pooled difference of $+25.1\%$ where a single patient supplied 1{,}824 of
5{,}564 cells. Recomputed per patient the difference is $+0.170$ with a 95\%
interval of $[-0.011, +0.342]$, containing zero. The direction survives
independent 1:1 within-patient depth matching at $+0.182$, so a direction with
support rather than an established effect.

\paragraph{Ambient contamination.} A sensitivity sweep at the committed 10\%
exclusion cap shows ambient signal manufacturing an intrinsic term of about 4.6\%
of a real compositional term, and never manufacturing a compositional one. The
asymmetry matters. Ambient contaminates the arm this work reports as unestimable,
not the arm it reports as measurable.
