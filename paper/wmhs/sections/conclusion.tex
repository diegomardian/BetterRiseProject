\iffull
Two of this paper's three statistics could not fail. A recovery curve in which
the estimator cancels still draws a smooth line; a grid that cannot resolve a
crossing still returns a stable number; a confound diagnostic whose tolerance
sits above its own attainable bound still reports clean. Each looked like a
measurement, and in every case the fix was to ask what the statistic was a
function of rather than to look harder at its output.
\fi

That question has a cheap answer for recovery curves specifically, and the
answer generalises past our instance: compare against the realised draw rather
than the requested parameter, and assert the difference is not identically zero.
It costs one line, it runs on the sweep you already have, and in a patient
simulator it separates two estimators whose curves agree to machine precision.
We would rather it had been in our design document than in our findings.
