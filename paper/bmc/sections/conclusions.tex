The contribution is not the colorectal observation. It is that executable
counterexamples expose validation checks which pass while the property they name
goes untested, together with a measured account of what that costs. In this
workflow the percentile interval is miscalibrated under every generator tested
except the saturated boundary; the cutpoint summary silently drops a coverage
crossing when a stricter criterion is absent; and the table resolver cannot say
which same-date run it read. Each is checkable, each was found by running code
against a committed input, and each has a small repair.

What a reader should take is the procedure: for every check, construct the input
that must make it fail, commit that input, and count a failure of the check as
the only success. Where a check cannot be made to fail, that is the finding. The
adenoma decomposition is the worked example that shows the procedure on a real
analysis, including the claims it retires.

Two things remain open and they are not equivalent. The descriptive estimates are
reportable only within the limits set out in Results, with their weighting and
denominator. And the audit is not yet validated, because the blinded challenge
that its own stop rule depends on has not been executed.
