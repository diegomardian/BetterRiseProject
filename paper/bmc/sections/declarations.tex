\section*{Declarations}

\subsection*{Ethics approval and consent to participate}
This work uses only publicly available, de-identified data that has previously
been published: the Vanderbilt/HTAN colorectal polyp atlas (HTAN Data Portal;
cBioPortal study \texttt{crc\_hta11\_htan\_2021}), the Pelka colorectal cancer
atlas (GSE178341), the two Lee cohorts (GSE132465 and GSE144735), and the
Crowell whole-transcriptome spatial deposit (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15574384). No
new human-subjects data were generated, no participant was contacted, and no
additional ethics approval was required.

\subsection*{Consent for publication}
Not applicable.

\subsection*{Availability of data and materials}
Raw data are available from the accessions above. Analysis code, derived
per-patient summaries and versioned result tables are released under the
policy the earlier source states: derived summaries and code, never cell-level
matrices, because single-cell genotypes remain re-identifiable in principle.
Every number in this paper resolves to a versioned table through the
reproduction index, whose entries are recorded as repository-relative paths so
that a fresh checkout resolves them; the index also lists the command that
regenerates each deliverable. The blinded-challenge deliverable that the
submission's stop rule depends on is present in the index as
\textsc{blocked}, not as done.

\subsection*{Competing interests}
The authors declare that they have no competing interests.
