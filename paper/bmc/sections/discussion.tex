\subsection{Objection 1: a change in selected cells is not silencing in
retained mature cells}
This is structurally unclosable with the available deposits. The spatial dataset
supplies no control gene independent of epithelial fraction; the paired chromatin
cohort fails the design; and the methylation control does not validate the
instrument on the cohort where the premise holds. We remove the causal claim
rather than defend it. What remains is a descriptive pattern in the selected,
depth-matched populations, and that is how it is reported.

\subsection{Objection 2: the positive result depends on post-hoc statistics,
labels and estimability rules}
Partially closable, and the closure is the sensitivity table. Every weighting,
both denominators, all four rungs and all four statistics are published with
patient counts and missingness, and the statistic disagreements are named rather
than averaged. At the rung where the descriptive claim is made, the load-bearing
statistic separates all eight cross-block contrasts from zero under every
weighting and denominator; agreement across all four statistics is six of eight
under doubly-robust and normal weighting and five of eight under tumour. No
retrospective specification, and no split of the same data, restores independent
confirmation; the split is reported as a split, not a replication.

\subsection{Objection 3: familiar testing advice, an internal case study,
overlapping workshop material}
Partially closable, and we do not overstate the closure. The reusable part is the
procedure and its executable examples; the blinded challenge that would extend it
beyond the fixtures used to build the checks is designed but not executed. The
workshop overlap is disclosed as a matrix rather than minimised: the adenoma
non-collapse, the grid analysis, the interval material and the simulator
experiments appear in the earlier workshop source and are not claimed as novel
here. Field-wide prevalence of these failure modes would need an external audit
and is not claimed.

\subsection{Threats to validity}
The descriptive adenoma evidence is one cohort pair; the two reproductions are
from derived inputs, not raw data; the resolver finding changes no committed
number but shows nothing prevented one; the inventory is one worker's
classification without inter-rater agreement; and the blinded challenge is
unexecuted, so the audit's own stop rule cannot be applied.
